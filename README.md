## Adding or changing a recipe

All recipes are in **`js/data.js`**. The top of that file explains every field. Recipes the head chef changes or adds in the book itself are kept in Firestore and laid over this file, and the book's copy wins (see Recipes changed in the book below). To add one here:

1. Copy an existing recipe block, from `{` to `},`.
2. Give it a new `id` (short, no spaces, for example `gravy`) and the next card number.
3. Fill in the name, yield, ingredients, steps and allergens.
4. To make an ingredient tappable, add `link: 'gravy'` (the other recipe's `id`).
5. Put anything that isn't confirmed yet in `[square brackets]` so it shows as a placeholder.
6. Allergens: tag each ingredient (`tags: ['Milk', 'Soy?']`) and leave out `contains` and `check`; the book works them out from the tags.
7. If it is a dish on the guest menu, add its `id` to the right section in `menuOrder` at the top of `js/data.js`, in the spot where the menu prints it. A dish left out of `menuOrder` still shows, under "Other dishes". Batch recipes and components are not listed there.
8. Change `updated` at the top of the file to today's date.

Since 27 Oct 2026 the mark sits inside the words, like Urgent, so it shows on a phone too (who wrote the line is hidden below 600 px) and never narrows the words; order, wastage and transfer lines, Done today and Ordered today have it the same way (`unsentMark()`, `snapLines()`, `patchOrDraw()` and `patchLines()` in `js/prep.js`). It shows only while the No signal or Not sent yet strip is up (`unsentOn()`, `stripOn()`): every write waits for the server's answer, even on a good signal, and a mark on every tick made the rows below jump under the next tap. While the strip is up every row keeps the mark's room, unseen and with no words in the row (`.nb-held`), so a tick with no signal only fills it: the rows move once, with the strip, and never under the next tap (measured at 390 px: a chef's Laksa moved the rows below 26 px, now 0). `tests/prep-speed.test.js`.
