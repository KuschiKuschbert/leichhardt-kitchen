# Leichhardt Kitchen Book

The Leichhardt Hotel kitchen's app: the recipe book, the prep list, the order list, the wastage log and the team's how-to guides, plus the head chef's admin panel. It works on any phone, tablet or computer with a web browser and can be installed as an app. Working rules for changing it are in `CLAUDE.md`.

## What it does

- **Two ways to look at the recipes.** The Pass opens first. The Pass / Tiles switch next to the search box changes it any time (and **Settings** sets which one opens):
  - **Tiles:** every recipe as a card with its small drawn dish, with search, recipe type and "Free from" filters on top.
  - **The Pass:** dishes drawn as plates and batch recipes as GN pans. Tap a plate to zoom in, then tap the laksa on the barramundi to open the laksa. The allergen lens shows where an allergen is at every level.
- **Linked batch recipes.** Any ingredient that has its own recipe is underlined orange with a "Recipe 06" tag, so tapping it opens that recipe. Batch recipes show which dishes they go on.
- **Allergens roll up.** A dish picks up the allergens of the batch recipes it uses, and names where each one comes from. A "?" means "may be in it, check the supplier label".
- **Cards to come.** A batch recipe that isn't in the book yet shows as "Card to come". Every dish that uses one is marked "Allergens incomplete", is left out of the Free from filter, and counts as "may have" under the allergen lens, until that card is added.
- **Set out like the guest menu.** Recipes are grouped in the order the menu prints them: Snackin' Plates, Salads, Big Country Feeds, Off the Grill, Toppers, Schnittys & Parmys, Hand Held, Sides, Dessert, Seniors and Kids, then the Functions platters. Tiles and The Pass both follow it, and each section is also a filter chip in Tiles. The Functions chip is a rounded rectangle and always sits first after All. Batch recipes and components come after the menu sections ("Bains and prep" on The Pass).
- **From the side.** Plated dishes also show a labelled side view, so the team can see what sits on top. Burgers show their build, top to bottom, instead.
- **Pin labels.** On the plate drawing the page places each label where it does not cover the food, other labels or other pins, with a thin line back to its pin. It reads the drawing to find the food, so a new dish needs no label tuning. Boards and platters (more than 8 pins) use smaller labels. The ring is see-through so the food shows through it.
- **Tick off steps** while cooking. The ticks clear when the page is closed.
- **Search (October 2026):**
  - Every word counts, in any order: "parmy chicken" finds Chicken Parmy.
  - "Gluten free", "gf", "no dairy", "df", "nut free", "egg free", "shellfish free" and so on show the recipes free from it, by the same rule as the Free from chips (no "may have", and no recipe with a card still to come). A note above the results says so. It also works with a word: "gf burger". So the Pass, which has the allergen lens instead of Free from chips, can do free-from too.
  - A word that is one slip away from a word in the book is put right when nothing else matches ("chiken" shows chicken, with a note saying so). Searches that still find nothing are queued for the admin panel as before.
  - The search bar stays at the top of the screen while the list scrolls. On phones the "16 of 114 match" count shows while a search or filter is on. Switching between the Pass and Tiles starts at the top of the list.
- **Recipe cards on phones and tablets:** the drawings come first, so a row under the name trail jumps straight to Allergens, Ingredients or Method. It is hidden on wide screens, where the card is in two columns.
- **Settings:**
  - Which view it opens with
  - Text size (Normal, Large, Extra large)
  - Clear ticked steps
  - Open on a phone (computers and wide tablets only, 900 px and wider): the QR code (`qr.svg`) that opens the book, with Print the A4 sign (`qr-sign.pdf`) and Download the QR code (`qr.png`)

  Settings are saved on each phone or tablet.

## Adding or changing a recipe

All recipes are in **`js/data.js`**. The top of that file explains every field. To add one:

1. Copy an existing recipe block, from `{` to `},`.
2. Give it a new `id` (short, no spaces, for example `gravy`) and the next card number.
3. Fill in the name, yield, ingredients, steps and allergens.
4. To make an ingredient tappable, add `link: 'gravy'` (the other recipe's `id`).
5. Put anything that isn't confirmed yet in `[square brackets]` so it shows as a placeholder.
6. Allergens: tag each ingredient (`tags: ['Milk', 'Soy?']`) and leave out `contains` and `check`; the book works them out from the tags.
7. If it is a dish on the guest menu, add its `id` to the right section in `menuOrder` at the top of `js/data.js`, in the spot where the menu prints it. A dish left out of `menuOrder` still shows, under "Other dishes". Batch recipes and components are not listed there.
8. Change `updated` at the top of the file to today's date.

## Testing

The `tests/` folder holds click tests that open the book in Chrome and use it like the team does, on a phone, a tablet and a computer. Only the tests need installing (Node.js and Google Chrome on the computer):

```
cd tests
npm install
npm test
```

- **What it checks:** every page and admin tab at 390, 768 and 1280 px (no sideways scroll, no errors), and the click paths for the prep list (with double ups), orders, wastage, minutes, shift pings, guides (with a real PDF read by pdf.js), the admin panel and the recipe search. About 430 checks, a few minutes.
- **No real data.** The prep pages normally talk to Firebase. The tests answer those requests with a small stand-in (`tests/fake/`) that keeps made-up people (Sam, Ann and Bo), prep lines, orders, wastage and two guides in the browser. No kitchen PIN, staff names or real lines are needed, and nothing is written to the real book. pdf.js is installed with the tests (`pdfjs-dist`) and served from there, so the PDF test needs no internet.
- **Any time of day.** Each page opens at 14:00 today, kitchen time, and the clock runs from there, so the 10:30 shift pings popup and the rest behave the same whenever the tests run. The tests and the browser run in Queensland time whatever zone the computer is set to. The update check's answer carries no `Date` in the tests, so the book keeps that 14:00; `clock.test.js` sets a wrong phone clock and zone (`clock`, `tz` and `server` in `L.open`) to check the book still uses the server's time.
- `npm test -- recipes` runs only the files with "recipes" in the name. `SHOTS=1 npm test -- pages` also saves a screenshot of every page to `tests/shots/`. `BASE=https://kuschikuschbert.github.io/leichhardt-kitchen/ npm test` runs the tests on the live site (Firebase is still the stand-in). `CHROME=/path/to/chromium` uses another browser.
- **Which pictures changed.** Before a change to the look, keep a copy of `tests/shots/` (for example as `tests/shots-baseline/`, which git ignores). After the change, `SHOTS=1 npm test -- pages` again, then `node tests/shots-diff.js tests/shots-baseline` lists every page whose picture is not the same as before, so only those need a look.
- `node tests/serve.js` serves the book at http://127.0.0.1:8777/ for trying it by hand.

## Good to know

- This repo is **public**, so anyone with the link can read the recipes. The page asks search engines not to list it.
- Allergens are worked out from the ingredients listed. Always check the supplier labels before answering a guest.
- Cook as written. Check with the head chef or sous chef before changing anything.
- **The book updates itself.** Every merge to `main` changes the site's `Last-Modified` and `ETag`. The book asks for just the headers of `index.html` (a tiny request, no Firestore) when it opens, when someone comes back to it and every 15 minutes while open. If they changed, it reloads: straight away when the app was brought back after 5 minutes or more away, otherwise once nobody has touched it for 2 minutes and nothing is being typed (an input in focus blocks it). Meanwhile a bar says "New version available" with an Update now button. It reloads at most once every 10 minutes, so it cannot loop. The 04:00 daily refresh stays as a backstop. A phone running a version from before this feature picks it up on its next reload (04:00 or reopening the app). No version number to bump and nothing to configure.
- **One clock.** Every time the book saves (written, ticked, ordered, removed, waste, sign-offs, guides) and every time it decides what day or hour it is (today's list, Waiting since, Closing soon, written after close, the 10:30 shift pings, 04:00) comes from the server's time in kitchen time (Queensland, UTC+10, no daylight saving). A phone's own clock can be minutes or hours out, or set to another zone. The answer to the update check above carries the server's time in its `Date` header, so the book keeps the gap on the phone (`lk.clock.gap`, kept for when there is no signal) and adds it. No extra requests and no Firestore reads. Before this, a phone 3 minutes slow could never undo its own mis-tap (the rules time the 2 minutes on the server). The rollovers wait until the clock has been checked on each visit (or 10 seconds), so a phone with the wrong date never rolls lines onto the wrong day. In the code: `LK_TIME` in `index.html`; `now()`, `dayNow()` and `T.day`, `T.hour`, `T.mins`, `T.dow`, `T.clock`, `T.at` in `js/prep.js`. `Date.now()` is only for time passing on the phone (timers, the PIN lock, the idle sign-out). Times are still plain milliseconds, so old data and the cached past days need nothing.

## Installing it as an app

- The logo is a chalk "KB": off-white handwriting (Caveat) with an orange underline on a dark specials board, chosen by the head chef from five options (18 Oct 2026). The letters are drawn as paths, so no font is needed. `icons/` holds the app icons (512 with "kitchen book" and a flame; 192, 180 and 96 with just KB; the maskable one drawn smaller for Android's circle crop), `icon.svg` is the browser tab version, and the same mark sits in the top bar (`LOGO_MARK` in `js/app.js`) and on the QR sign. Phones that installed the app earlier may need it removed and added again to show the new icon (iPhone always does).
- The book is an installable web app: `manifest.webmanifest` (name, colours, icons in `icons/`, shortcuts to Prep list, Orders, Wastage and Recipes on a long press of the icon) and `sw.js` (service worker).
- Phones and tablets that have not installed the book yet see an **Install the app** card at the top of the landing page (18 Oct 2026). In Chrome, Edge and Samsung Internet it has an Install button; on iPhone and iPad it explains Share, then Add to Home Screen; elsewhere it points to the browser menu. "Not now" hides it on that device only (`installNo` in the saved settings). It never shows in the installed app or on computers.
- Settings has the same **Install the app** section: an Install button in Chrome, Edge and Samsung Internet; on iPhone and iPad it explains Share, then Add to Home Screen; on a Mac in Safari, File, Add to Dock.
- The browser's install offer (`beforeinstallprompt`) is caught by a small script in `index.html` before the app scripts load, and `preventDefault()` is called on it, because Chrome only opens its install box from our button once that has been done. If the box fails to open, the card and Settings fall back to the manual steps. The manifest `id` is `/leichhardt-kitchen/`, so the app has its own identity on `kuschikuschbert.github.io` and cannot clash with another app on the same address.
- The service worker is network first for the book's own files, so the newest cards load whenever there is a connection, and keeps the last copy so the recipe book still opens with no signal. Google Fonts are kept once loaded. Firebase (prep list, orders, wastage) is not touched; it keeps its own offline copy. To force everyone onto a fresh cache, change `CACHE` in `sw.js`.

## Prep book and admin panel

- Staff only see today in `#/prep` (no back, forward or date picker); the head chef and sous chefs can still browse other days. Unfinished lines roll over to today by themselves. This is the page only: past days stay readable in the database to anyone with the kitchen PIN.
- `#/` is the landing page (five dockets: Recipes, Prep list, Orders, Wastage, and Guides as the long docket under the other four). The recipes live at `#/recipes`, the order list at `#/orders`, the wastage log at `#/waste`. A pill menu (big screens) or a bottom tab bar (phones) switches between them; tapping "Kitchen Book" goes back to the landing page.
- `#/prep` is the prep book, laid out as an open notebook: today on the left, yesterday on the right (two pages on a big screen, stacked on a phone). Staff enter the kitchen PIN once per phone, pick their name, and the first time choose their own 4-digit PIN. That phone then stays signed in.
- Sections are not preset. Whoever writes a line types a section name (or picks one already used) and it appears as a section on the page and as a tab. The app keeps track of sections in the background and offers them as suggestions.
- Ticking, writing and removing lines is counted per person in the background.
- `#/admin` is the admin panel: who is writing and ticking most, the sections people have written (hide one from suggestions), names, roles and permissions, and Reset PIN for a person. It needs Google sign-in as the project owner.
- Data lives in Firebase Firestore (free Spark plan): `config/main`, `days/<date>/items`, `staffpins/<person>` (hashed, unreadable; only a person listed in `config/main.staffIds` can set one, so nobody with just the kitchen PIN can make up a writer; the head chef may also write one, for the move to a new kitchen PIN), `pinset/<person>`, `proofs/<person>` (write only), `activity/<person>`, `sections/<slug>`, `orders/<date>/lines`, `waste/<date>` and `waste/<date>/lines`, `notes/<id>` (shift pings), `minutes/<id>` (meeting minutes), `learned/<item>` and `ordlearned/<item>` (what the prep and order lists learned), `learnlog/<date>`, `misses/<slug>` (searches that found nothing), `views/<recipe>` (cards opened), `guides/<id>` and `guides/<id>/pages/<n>` (see Guides). Firebase config is in `js/firebase-config.js` (public by design). The kitchen PIN and admin email live in the published Firestore rules, not in this repo. Staff names are not in the repo either.
- Personal PINs are the write lock. Anyone with the kitchen PIN can read, but every write must carry the writer's own 4-digit PIN, which the Firestore rules check against the stored hash (see Staff lock). Per-person permissions (add, tick, delete) are still guidance in the page. A person's tally in `activity` is written only by themselves, the head chef or a sous chef; `sections`, `learned`, `misses` and `views` take only their known fields from a PIN holder, and only the head chef or a sous chef can hide a section or a learned item. Section and amount fields are capped at 40 characters. Items are never hard deleted.
- Changing the kitchen PIN means editing the Firestore rules and re-entering it on every phone.

### Roles, rollover, insights, badges and recipe links

- **Head chef (owner):** signs in with Google at `#/admin` and sees everything: charts and numbers for 7, 14 or 30 days (lines per day, by person, time of day, rolled over most often, sections, weekday load, slow and sticky lines, empty searches), the Team and Waste tabs, the tallies, people and permissions, PIN resets and the badge switch.
- **Sous chef:** no Google needed. Set a person's role to Sous chef in the admin panel. They sign in with their name and PIN like everyone else, and get a "Manage staff" button on the Handy page to add and remove people. The Firestore rules only let a PIN holder change the `people` list of `config/main` and `staffIds` (who may set a PIN); nothing else in that document. Only people listed in `sousIds` (kept in step by the owner panel) may change it. The rules cannot tell a name change from a role change, so if a sous chef's save marks someone Sous chef or ticks them for Pings, the admin panel shows "Changed from the prep book" at the top with Keep these changes and Undo them; nothing takes effect until the head chef keeps it.
- **Pings:** a tick in the admin People tab lets one person write shift pings from Handy without making them a sous chef (see Shift pings). Sous chefs always can.
- **New pages (holding pages back from staff):** under New pages in the admin People tab the head chef can untick **Staff see Guides**, **Staff see Shift pings** and **Staff see Meeting minutes** and save. An unticked page is hidden from Staff and View only: no Guides docket, Guides tab or guide links on recipe cards and in the search, no Pings bell or popup, no Minutes not signed yet card and no Meeting minutes button in Handy. Opening `#/guides` or `#/minutes` directly says the page is not open to everyone yet. The head chef, sous chefs and leads always see all three, and anyone ticked for Pings always sees shift pings. Nothing saved yet means everyone sees everything. It is kept in `config/main` (`staffSee`) and only hides links and skips reads, it is not a lock. Each phone remembers what its person may see (`lk.feat`), so the landing page is right before the book connects.
- **Reads kept small.** Past days are read once a day per phone and kept on the phone: prep (`lk.past`), order history (`lk.ord.hist`), recent waste (`lk.w.recent`, read again after a past entry is removed) and the waste review note (`lk.w.note`). Everything to order reads the last 14 days once (at most once a minute) and then keeps itself in step: today's lines come from the live list and a tick or remove on an older line is applied on the phone.
- **Rollover:** the first person to open today's book writes every line that was not ticked on the last written day (up to 7 days back) onto today, marked "Rolled" (with "x2" and so on for repeats) and sorted to the top of its section. Each rolled line stores `rolled`, `root`, `from` and `rolledFrom`, so the Insights page can show how often and which lines roll over, plus `origBy` and `origAt`: who first wrote it and when (`origAt` is 0 on lines that rolled before October 2026, when it was not kept yet). There is no Yesterday page any more.
- **Written for tomorrow.** A line written after the usual close (the time of the last tick on most days, worked out from the past 14 days; 20:00 until the book knows) is meant for the next day. When it moves onto that day it looks like a normal line (no tag, the writer's name as usual), and it does not count in Rolled, Stuck, Start here, the CSV or the Insights rolled numbers. Only if it is still open after that day does it show as Rolled (once). The same goes for order lines: a late order line waits "Since" the next day. Data: `ahead: true` on the copy (kept as it rolls on); `rolled` still counts the copies, so learning and timings never count a line twice. The prep rollover waits for the past days to load, so it knows the usual close. New copies also keep `origAt` (when the original was written). Lines that rolled over before this rule (or on a phone with older code) are repaired: the first phone to open today's list checks each line that rolled once against its original in the cached past days, and marks it `ahead` for everyone if it was written after the usual close.
- **Urgent:** the ! button on a line pins it above the rest.
- **Double ups merge (October 2026).** The same item open twice on today's list, in any section, is one job. Lines are the same item when their words match, ignoring case, spacing, punctuation and plurals ("Slice onions" and "slice onion."); amounts agree when they are the same ("2 L" and "2l") or one is blank. Writing an item that is already open (typed, Add from the recipes or Quick add) adds nothing: the open line takes the amount if it had none and the section written, and the toast says "Pick coriander is already on the list under Larder" with Add anyway. Double ups already on the list (a line rolled over and written again, two phones at once, older lists) merge by themselves a moment after the list opens: the line written first stays (a rolled line before a new one) and takes the amount, the urgent mark, a section and a recipe link if it had none; the others are removed with `mergedInto`, and the toast has Undo. The line kept shows "Also Fryer" for the other sections it was written under and shows on their tabs too (`also`). The same item with different amounts is not merged: each line shows "Also Prep 5 kg". A ticked line is never a double up (a second batch is a new job). Add anyway, Undo and Put back keep a line apart for good (`dupOk`). Before merging, the phone waits a moment at random (so usually one phone does it) and reads just those lines from the server (`getDocFromServer`, a few reads, only when there is a double up), so a phone with an old copy of the list never removes a line ticked somewhere else. Each group is tried once per page load. Merged lines do not count as "Often removed" in Insights. In the code: `dupKey`, `mergeDups`, `openTwin` in `js/prep.js`; tests in `tests/double-ups.test.js`.
- **Start here:** with 4 or more open lines today, a card at the top of the list picks up to 3 to do first: urgent lines, then the longest rolled-over, then lines that usually take 2 hours or more (last 7 days), then the ones waiting longest. Tapping one jumps to it. On phones and tablets the card sits above the lines (`.nb-start-l`); on screens 900 px and wider it sits at the top of the Handy page (`.nb-start-r`) so it does not push the lines down. Only one of the two shows.
- **More lines on big screens (900 px and wider):** the notebook page uses the screen height (the old 780 px cap is gone), the left page is wider than Handy (3 to 2), the add row is one line (item, section, amount, Add), the empty hint line is hidden, and with a mouse (`hover: hover`, `pointer: fine`) lines are 40 px high and section headings 32 px. Phones and touch tablets keep the large rows. On short screens (860 px high or less) the dark top bar is 96 px instead of 132 px. At 1920x1080 about 11 lines show where 5 showed before (mock data with six sections).
- **Usual amounts:** while typing a line, the amounts written for it before show as chips ("Usual amount: 2 tubs"). One-tap adds carry the usual amount when one clearly wins. Amounts are kept in `learned/<item>.qtys`.
- **Waste review note:** the latest "change for the next shift" from the last 3 days of waste reviews shows at the top of today's list.
- **Ticked lines leave the list.** Once a line is ticked it disappears from the prep list and shows only under Done today in Handy, so the list stays short. A section with nothing left to do is hidden. When everything is ticked the page says so. Ticking shows a short "Ticked ... Undo" message for mis-taps. The same rules apply as before (the ticker for 2 minutes, head chef and sous chefs any time), now through the Undo button in Done today. The header count ("2 of 4 done"), the section counts, Copy the list and Print the list still include ticked lines.
- **Add from the recipes.** The button opens a panel that floats over the list, so the list and the add box do not move or shrink. Nothing is listed until you type a name or tap Show all. The list inside scrolls. Escape, Close or adding a recipe closes it.
- **Order list: lines roll over until ordered.** Like the prep list, anything not ticked as ordered on the last written day is copied onto today's order list by itself, every day, until someone ticks it as ordered (removed lines do not roll). A rolled line shows "Since" and the day it was first written, and "from" the person who wrote it. Each phone tries once a day (`lk.ord.rolled`), the copy's id comes from the first line (`r<date>-<root>`), so two phones never make two copies. Everything to order, the regulars chips and the admin order stats count each rolled item once (its newest copy). Data: `rolled` (times rolled), `root`, `from`, `rolledFrom`, `since`, `origBy`, `origAt` (when it was first written; the Team tab's time from written to ordered counts from it).
- **Order list: waiting more than 3 days.** On today's list, lines not ordered more than 3 days after they were first written sit together at the top under "Waiting more than 3 days", grouped by the day they were written ("Written Wed 15 Oct, 4 days ago"). When the order list opens, a "Still not ordered" nudge says how many (head chef and sous chefs: order them or remove them; staff: tell the head chef or a sous chef), with Show them and Hide for today (`lk.ord.nudge`).
- **Order list: it learns.** Every order line written is remembered for good in `ordlearned/<item>` (`text`, `uses`, `dow` weekday counts, `qtys` usual amounts, `lastUsed`, `lastBy`, `hidden`). The nightly learning counts each finished day (see Nightly learning); the phone writes nothing, it only keeps its own copy in step so the next suggestion already knows. The list is read once a day per phone (`lk.ord.learned`, the 300 most used). It drives the suggestions while typing (most used first, with the usual amount), a Usual amount chip once the name is typed, the "Usually ordered" chips (items ordered 3 or more times on this weekday), and the proofreader's word list. Needs the `ordlearned` rules; until they are published the order list suggests from the last 14 days only.
- **Order list: ordered lines move to the bottom.** On today's order list a line stays in the list for 10 minutes after it is ticked as ordered (so a mis-tap is easy to undo), then it leaves the list and shows under "Ordered today" at the bottom, with the time, who ordered it and an Undo button for the head chef and sous chefs. Every line is in one place only. The list belongs to the day, so it is empty again tomorrow; the By day view still shows any past day in full, and "Everything to order" only ever shows lines not yet ordered. The "2 of 3 ordered" count includes the moved lines.
- **Order list layout.** The "What do we need to order?" box is at the top of the page so it is always in reach. On wide screens (1000 px and up) the page is wider: the list on the left and "Ordered today" on the right, side by side under the add box. On phones everything stacks: add box, list, Ordered today. Everything to order stays a single column.

- Every line written or picked is counted in `kitchens/<pin>/learned/<item>` by the nightly learning (see below): total uses, typed versus picked (a line with a recipe counts as picked), uses per weekday (`dow`, 0 is Sunday), per section and usual amounts. Removed lines and rolled-over copies are not counted. New lines show in what was learned the next morning.
- While typing, matching learned items (and batch and component recipe names) show as tap-to-fill chips under the box. A learned item also fills in its usual section when the section box is empty.
- Quick add on the Handy page uses the learned items for "Usual for a <weekday>" (written on at least two of that weekday) and "Regulars" (4 or more uses, `REGULAR` in `js/prep.js`), after Due today.
- Owner insights: items learned, regulars, share added by tapping a suggestion, most written items, a weekday heat table, and a manage table to hide typos. The server reads past lists by itself, up to 90 days back (see below).

### Proofreader

- Every new written line is tidied (spacing, punctuation, capital first letter) and spell-checked before it is saved, in the browser, no service needed.
- A word is only corrected when it is not already known and exactly one known word is one slip away (two for long words). Known words come from recipe names and ingredients, a kitchen word list in `js/prep.js` (`KITCHEN`), and everything the list has learned, so new dish names stop being touched once they have been written.
- A corrected line keeps what was typed in `orig`. A toast offers Undo, and an undone word is left alone on that device. The owner sees the latest corrections in Insights.

- **Sections are spell-checked too.** A section one slip away from a section already in use (two for long names) becomes that section, so "Fryre" goes under "Fryer" instead of starting a new one. Singular and plural are the same section ("Salad" goes under "Salads"). A real word is never swapped for a different section ("Pans" stays "Pans" even when "Pass" exists). Otherwise longer words are checked like a line and the first letter becomes a capital. It happens when you leave the section box (toast with Undo) and again when the line is saved; an undone section is left alone on that device (`lk.prep.keepsec`). The wastage log's section box works the same way.

### Nightly learning (on the server)

- Every night at 03:00 kitchen time a GitHub Action (`.github/workflows/nightly-learning.yml`) runs `scripts/nightly-learn.mjs`. It reads every finished kitchen day (Brisbane time) in the last 90 that has not been learned yet, prep lines and order lines, and adds them to `learned/<item>` and `ordlearned/<item>`. The phones never write learning counts (only the head chef hiding an item), and never read past days to learn.
- A day is claimed in `learnlog/<date>` (prep) and `learnlog/o-<date>` (orders) in the same batch as its counts, so a day is never counted twice or half counted. Removed lines and rolled-over copies are not counted. The newest use wins for "last used", so a catch-up of an old day never makes it go backwards.
- It needs two repository secrets (Settings, Secrets and variables, Actions): `FIREBASE_SA`, a Firebase service account key (JSON, from the Firebase console: Project settings, Service accounts, Generate new private key), and `KITCHEN_PIN`. The run prints only how many days and lines it learned. Run it by hand from the Actions tab with Run workflow.
- Test it on the Firestore emulator: `cd scripts && npm install`, then `firebase emulators:exec --only firestore --project demo-test "node emulator-check.mjs"` (seeds two days, runs it twice, checks the counts).

### Checking the rules
- `tests/rules/` checks `firestore.rules` on the Firestore emulator with made-up values in place of the PIN and the email: `cd tests/rules && npm install && npm test` (Java is needed for the emulator; on a Mac with Homebrew's openjdk, `PATH=/opt/homebrew/opt/openjdk@17/bin:$PATH npm test`). It covers the right and wrong PIN, a spoofed `by`, a replayed proof, who may set a PIN, what a sous chef may change in `config/main`, tallies, pings, the size caps, the field lists and the guide pictures. Run it before publishing any rules change, and add a check for every new rule.
- Before 20 October 2026 the phones did this themselves; days they already counted are claimed in `learnlog` and skipped.

### Security setup

- `firestore.rules` is the rules template. The live rules are pasted into the Firebase console with the real kitchen PIN and owner email filled in. Never commit those values.
- The Firebase web key in `js/firebase-config.js` is public by design. Restrict it in Google Cloud (Credentials, the Browser key) to the site address, and turn on App Check (reCAPTCHA v3) so only the real site can talk to the database. To switch App Check on in the page, add `appCheckKey: '<reCAPTCHA v3 site key>'` to `js/firebase-config.js`.
- The owner panel has a Backup button (Tools) that downloads everything as one JSON file: people, prep lines, orders, wastage and end-of-day reviews from the last 400 days, shift pings and who has seen them (`notes`, `notesSeen`), meeting minutes with their earlier versions (`minutesHist`) and sign-offs (`minutesSigns`), what the prep and order lists have learned (`learned`, `ordlearned`), tallies, empty searches, card opens and the guides' words. People's PIN fingerprints and the guides' page pictures are left out. The free Firebase plan has no scheduled backups. A backup reads one small query per shift ping and per minutes on top of the collections, which is fine now and then.
- Who is the owner: only the owner's Google account may read `config/admins`, so the page checks that read. No email is stored in the database or the repository.

### Staff lock
- **PIN entry.** The kitchen PIN and a person's own PIN go in by themselves as soon as the fourth digit is typed. When choosing a new PIN, the cursor moves to "Type it again" after the first four digits and sends after the second four. Wrong tries still count toward the lock.
- **Proofreader.** It runs on prep lines, order lines and wastage items. It never changes a word it already knows, the singular or plural of a known word, a past tense of one, or a word with an accent (jalapeño). Orders and wastage now tell the writer what was corrected (prep still has the Undo button). Add everyday words to `KITCHEN` in `js/prep.js` when it wrongly changes one.
- **Desktop idle lock.** On a computer (mouse and keyboard), 7.5 minutes with no mouse, key or touch sends the prep pages back to the person's own PIN screen. The kitchen PIN is not asked again. Phones and tablets are not timed. A page that was left closed for longer than that also asks for the own PIN when it opens. It uses a timer and a timestamp on the device (`lk.prep.seen`), no reads or writes. Change `IDLE_MS` in `js/prep.js`.
- Each write sends the writer's id (`by`) and, in the same batch, `proofs/<id>` with `tok` (a hash of the person's own PIN) and `at` (a server timestamp). The rules compare `tok` with `staffpins/<id>.hash`, which nobody can read, using `getAfter`, and require `at` to equal the request time, so a proof left over from an earlier write cannot be reused. A wrong or missing PIN is refused by the server.
- The first person to set a PIN for a name claims it. The owner can reset it (Reset PIN) and it is chosen again.
- Limits: someone with the kitchen PIN could still guess a 4-digit PIN online by script (the page makes people wait after 3 wrong tries, 1 to 30 minutes and kept on the phone, but a script skips the page; only App Check or a paid server function can truly rate limit), and add, tick and delete rights per person are checked in the page only.

### Changing the kitchen PIN

1. Add a second copy of the `kitchens/<pin>/...` rules for the new 4 digit PIN to the live rules (see `firestore.rules`).
2. Owner panel, "Move to a new 4 digit kitchen PIN" (head chef only: the admin panel needs the head chef's Google account, the code checks again, and the new PIN only works once its rules are published in the Firebase console; a second tap confirms): copies the people, PIN fingerprints (each person's `pinset` record first; with the rules from 19 Oct 2026 the head chef may copy a fingerprint as it is, so a stored PIN for someone no longer on the list does not stop the move), prep lines, order lines, wastage and reviews (400 days), learned items, empty searches, tallies, shift pings and their seen records, minutes with their earlier versions and sign-offs, what the order list has learned, and guides with their page pictures to the new PIN, then switches that device over. Copying sign-offs and seen records needs the rules from 19 Oct 2026 (the head chef may create them); with older rules they stay on the old PIN and the message says how many.
3. Everyone else is asked for the PIN next time they open the book once the old rules are removed. Remove the old PIN's rules last, so nothing is cut off while moving.
