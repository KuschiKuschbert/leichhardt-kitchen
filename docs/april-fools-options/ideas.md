# April Fools on Home: the idea list

17 November 2026. The head chef's brief: "like a hacker has hacked the page and broken all elements; a super weird animation that says their name; dark and cruel so they think they broke something; hacker style, psychedelic; elements of the real design move around and break."

Every idea below uses the real Home's own pieces and the person's name, and ends with the same reveal (`designs.md`). The first three are drawn and playable in `preview/index.html`. The rest are not built; any of them can replace a step in a design or become its own design.

| # | Idea | What breaks | How it uses the name | Built |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Collapse.** The screen cracks like glass, every card swings off its pin and falls into a pile, the screen goes dark and types to you | The layout, physically | "hello ann." in his bubble, "ann. what did you press?" in the dark | A |
| 2 | **Takeover.** The screen switches off like an old telly; a hacker terminal deletes the recipes section by section, blames a coworker, counts down; FATAL ERROR 0x0401 becomes 1 APRIL | The whole screen, replaced | "user: ANN", "HELLO ANN.", "telling Bo it was Ann" | B |
| 3 | **Possessed.** The page stays but every word turns on you (Pants, Your breakdown, Disorders, Prep by sedation), letters melt, colours swirl, the page tears; each tap makes it worse | Words and colours, in place | "I see you, Ann.", everyone on with you is Ann | C |
| 4 | **Ransom note.** Letters are cut out of the page's own words, one by one, and fly together into a note in mismatched letters: "WE HAVE YOUR SAUCES, ANN. LEAVE 1 KG OF BUTTER AT THE PASS." | The words, rearranged | In the note | No |
| 5 | **The hungry mascot.** He walks across Home and eats the cards one by one (they shrink into his mouth), burps, and says "delicious, Ann. you're next." | The cards, eaten | His speech bubble | No |
| 6 | **Code rain.** Every letter on Home comes loose and drains down the screen in falling columns of characters; in the rain, the person's name lights up and stays | Every letter | Spelled out in the rain | No |
| 7 | **Black hole.** A black hole opens in the middle of Your day and pulls every card, tab and button into it, spinning; the name orbits it, getting smaller | Everything, sucked in | Orbiting the hole | No |
| 8 | **Upside-down gravity.** Everything falls up and sticks under the top bar; a note says "turn your phone over, Ann"; when they do (or tap), it falls the right way | The layout, the other way | In the note | No |
| 9 | **Endless Home.** Home zooms into the KB logo, which holds a smaller Home, which holds a smaller Home, faster and faster, until the name fills the screen | The page, repeated forever | Fills the screen at the end | No |
| 10 | **Dust.** The page breaks into big squares that blow away like dust from the top down, leaving black and a blinking line: "ann? are you still there?" | The page, into pieces | Typed in the black | No |
| 11 | **The book talks back.** Nothing moves; the mascot's bubble starts answering things the person did not say ("No, Ann." "Stop touching me." "I know what you did to the jus.") and the cards slowly turn away from them | The mood | In every line | No |

Not proposed, and why: anything that fakes a real security alert, police, a firing, a pay problem or stolen personal details (it threatens the person, not the book); a fake update or error from a real company's product (it copies someone else's look); anything on Prep, Orders, Wastage or Transfers (work pages stay true); fast strobing or full-screen red (photosensitivity, and red means a real error in the book).
