Spread the days

One tap proposed these. Nothing is saved until you confirm. Untick a line to leave that job alone, or tap its day to choose another.

The rules, in the order they win:
  1. Friday and Saturday take light jobs only, and at most a tenth of the week (1 of 2 used).
  2. No section gets two heavy jobs on one day.
  3. Jobs of one kind share a day across sections.
  4. Each section's week is as even as it can be.
  5. A job keeps its day unless a rule above moves it.
