How the days are chosen
  1. Friday and Saturday take light jobs only, and at most a tenth of the week. A job with no weight is never light.
  2. No section gets two heavy jobs on one day.
  3. With the switch on, jobs of one kind share a day, unless that makes a section's day too heavy.
  4. Each section's week is as even as it can be.
  5. A job keeps its day unless a rule above moves it.
