Plan the days

The book proposes. Nothing is saved until you press Save. Nightly jobs, the numbered Pack down steps and the general list are never moved.

Example only. The weights and kinds on this page were made up to show the planner working. Yours will change the days.

Jobs of one kind on the same day
Off: every job that has a day keeps it, and new jobs go on a light day for their section. On: shelves on Monday, fridges on Wednesday, behind equipment on Tuesday, and a less even week. Changing this after a save does not put days back.

Every few days not planned

A job every 2 or 3 days falls on a different weekday each time, so it has no day to choose and will reach Friday and Saturday whatever its weight. If a section has two, the book starts them on different days. Your lists hold 1: Change grill drip trays (every 2 days, a Pack down step, never moved).

How the days are chosen
  1. Friday and Saturday take light jobs only, and at most a tenth of the week. A job with no weight is never light.
  2. A job that has a day is never moved onto Friday or Saturday.
  3. No section gets two heavy jobs on one day.
  4. With the switch on, jobs of one kind share a day, unless that makes a section's day too heavy.
  5. Each section's week is as even as it can be.
  6. With the switch off, a job that has a day always keeps it.
