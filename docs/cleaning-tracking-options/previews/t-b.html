How this is counted, so it is fair:
  • A person counts only for days the plan had them on that section.
  • A missed job belongs to everyone who was on that section that day, not to one person.
  • Shared kitchen jobs belong to nobody.
  • A job marked Unable to complete, with its reason, is not a miss.
  • Missed means not ticked by midnight.
  • Nobody is ranked. Names are in alphabetical order.
