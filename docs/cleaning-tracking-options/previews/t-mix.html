How this is counted
  • A missed job belongs to its section. Shared kitchen jobs are counted, with no names.
  • Missed means not ticked by midnight on its day.
  • A job marked Unable to complete, with its reason, is not a miss and is not in the totals.
  • Counting starts the first night after Cleaning is switched on.
  • Nobody is ranked and nobody has a total.
To settle before the morning flag is switched on
  • A close that finishes after midnight would be flagged though the job was done.
  • "On at close" needs one rule for when the plan and a cook's own pick differ, and for a cook sent home early.
  • A job the chef typed in has no Unable to complete yet: it is tick or flag.
  • A night is counted once, at 03:00. A plan corrected later does not change it.
