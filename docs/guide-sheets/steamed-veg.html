Steamed veg on the iCombi

The steamed veg programme on the iCombi (the Rational): steam, 100 °C. Carrots, cauliflower and broccoli go in together and come out at their own beep, straight into ice water.

Steam, 100 °C. Fill a sink with ice water first. Times count from when the veg goes in.

  1. Ready beepAll inCarrots, cauliflower and broccoli, each in its own tray
  2. 2 minBroccoli outInto the ice water
  3. 4 minCauliflower outInto the ice water
  4. 6 minCarrots outLast beep. Into the ice water

Cook times: broccoli 2 min, cauliflower 4 min, carrots 6 min.

Once it is all cold: drain the water and put the veg in a big white tub with a lid.

Steps

  1. Fill one sink with ice water before you start, with plenty of ice.
  2. Prep the veg as on the Seasonal Vegetables card. Each vegetable goes in its own steam tray.
  3. Start the steamed veg programme: steam, 100 °C. The oven heats up and beeps once when it is ready.
  4. Ready beep: put all three trays in together. Carrots, cauliflower and broccoli. Close the door.
  5. Beep at 2 min: broccoli out, straight into the ice water.
  6. Beep at 4 min: cauliflower out, straight into the ice water.
  7. Last beep, 6 min: carrots out, straight into the ice water. The ice water stops the cooking.
  8. Leave all the veg in the ice water until it is cold.
  9. Drain the water. Put the veg in a big white tub with a lid.
