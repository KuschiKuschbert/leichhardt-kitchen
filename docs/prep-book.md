# Prep book and admin panel

Moved out of `README.md` on 3 November 2026 so sessions can read it in parts (README had grown past 350 KB). How the prep list, order list, wastage, transfers, pings, minutes, mail, to-do, drinks, sections, cut-offs, recipes and drawings in the book, functions, guides, the nightly jobs, the security setup and the staff lock work, section by section. Search for a heading and read that section only.

- Staff only see today in `#/prep` (no back, forward or date picker); the head chef and sous chefs can still browse other days. Unfinished lines roll over to today by themselves. Operational reads require a current personal PIN session and the applicable role. Public recipes and drawings remain available without a kitchen login. See [the access contract](private-kitchen-access.md).
- `#/` is the landing page (six dockets: Recipes, Prep list, Orders, Wastage, then Transfers and Guides as the long dockets under the other four; on a phone the Transfers docket keeps to one line so Guides still ends above the tab bar). The recipes live at `#/recipes`, the order list at `#/orders`, the wastage log at `#/waste`, the transfer list at `#/transfers`. A pill menu (big screens) or a bottom tab bar (phones) switches between them; tapping "Kitchen Book" goes back to the landing page.
- `#/prep` is the prep book, laid out as an open notebook: today's list on the left, the Handy page on the right (two pages on a big screen, stacked on a phone). Staff enter the kitchen PIN once per phone, pick their name, and the first time choose their own 4-digit PIN. Personal devices unlock with the same person's PIN each day at 04:00 Queensland time; shared screens also lock after 10 minutes idle. See [device sessions](device-sessions.md). Getting the book going (21 Oct 2026): on a phone with the kitchen PIN, the prep pages connect to Firebase straight away and every other page starts it 0.9 s after it is drawn, so Prep, Orders and Wastage open quickly. The kitchen PIN screen already downloads the Firebase files (about 180 kB) while the PIN is typed, with a plain fetch so a failed download on weak wifi cannot stop the real start after Open (`sdkEarly()` in `js/prep.js`, `tests/pin-keyboard.test.js`).
- Sections are not preset. Whoever writes a line types a section name (or picks one already used) and it appears as a section on the page and as a tab. The app keeps track of sections in the background and offers them as suggestions.
- Ticking, writing and removing lines is counted per person in the background.
- `#/admin` holds Review desk, Reports, Saved records, Operations and Settings. Admin access requires the owner's verified Google identity, this device's live head-chef PIN session and its matching device grant. Remembered Google alone grants no operational access.
- Data lives in Firebase Firestore (free Spark plan): `config/main`, `days/<date>/items`, `staffpins/<person>` (hashed, unreadable; only a person listed in `config/main.staffIds` can set one, so nobody with just the kitchen PIN can make up a writer; the head chef may also write one, for the move to a new kitchen PIN), `pinset/<person>`, `proofs/<person>` (the older PIN proof, switched off in phase 2: nothing writes it), `tries/<person>` (the last PIN try, write only) and `sessions/<phone's sign-in uid>` (who that phone writes as, read only by that phone; see PIN sessions under Staff lock), `activity/<person>`, `sections/<slug>`, `orders/<date>/lines`, `waste/<date>` and `waste/<date>/lines`, `transfers/<date>/lines` (see Transfers between venues), `notes/<id>` (shift pings), `minutes/<id>` (meeting minutes), `learned/<item>` and `ordlearned/<item>` (what the prep and order lists learned), `learnlog/<date>`, `misses/<slug>` (searches that found nothing), `views/<recipe>` (cards opened), `guides/<id>` and `guides/<id>/pages/<n>` (see Guides), `drinks/<person>` (see Drinks run), `shifts/<date>_<person>` (see Kitchen sections), `todos/<id>` (see Chef to-do), `cutdone/<date>_<supplier>` (the Supplier orders ticks), `push/<hash>` and `pushsent/<date>` (phone pushes; see Order cut-offs), `book/index`, `recipes/<id>` with `recipes/<id>/hist/<v>` (see Recipes changed in the book) and `drawings/<id>` with its `hist` (see Drawing plates in the book). Firebase config is in `js/firebase-config.js` (public by design). The kitchen PIN and admin email live in the published Firestore rules, not in this repo. Staff names are not in the repo either.
- Personal PINs unlock operational reading and writing for the current person and role. The kitchen PIN opens only the minimal name picker. Operational requests use this browser's anonymous device identity; admin additionally needs the matching owner grant: the Firestore rules check that phone's PIN session (since 22 Oct 2026; see Staff lock). Since 22 Oct 2026 the rules also check who writes a line and when: a new prep or order line is written as its writer (`addedById`, or empty for a rolled copy), a tick is the ticker's own (`doneById`), staff write only yesterday's, today's and tomorrow's lists (the head chef, sous chefs and function managers write any day), only the head chef or a sous chef ticks an order line as ordered or takes the tick off, and a line holds at most 60 fields. Add, Tick and Delete use the owner's saved canonical capability lists on the page and server. A sous chef's requested ticks take effect only after the owner keeps them. Delivery temperatures remain available to every valid personal login. Function editing, generated task effects and recent own-add Undo retain their separate checks. See [staff capabilities](staff-capabilities.md). A person's tally in `activity` is written only by themselves, the head chef or a sous chef; `sections`, `misses` and `views` take only their known fields from a PIN holder; `learned`, `ordlearned` and `learnlog` are written only by the nightly job (and the verified admin), and a phone may only hide or show an item, as the head chef or a sous chef. A sous chef's save of the staff list always keeps the head chef, the sous chefs and the function managers on `staffIds`. A history copy (`hist/<v>` of a recipe, drawing or function) can only be written in the save that makes version v + 1. Section and amount fields are capped at 40 characters. Items are never hard deleted.
- Changing the kitchen PIN means editing the Firestore rules and re-entering it on every phone.

### Roles, rollover, insights, badges and recipe links

- **Head chef (owner):** the verified admin opens `#/admin` with the current head-chef PIN and device grant. The selected Chef's Desk groups Dashboard, Review desk, Reports, Saved records, Operations and Settings. The head-chef PIN retains its kitchen role without granting admin access by itself.
- **Sous chef:** no Google needed. Set a person's role to Sous chef in the admin panel. They sign in with their name and PIN like everyone else, and get **Manage staff** in the Chef group of the chef mark menu to add and remove people. The Firestore rules only let a PIN holder change the `people` list of `config/main` and `staffIds` (who may set a PIN); nothing else in that document. Only people listed in `sousIds` (kept in step by the owner panel) may change it. The staff page has no role controls, but the rules cannot tell a name change from a role change, so if the roles in the people list ever disagree with the lists (a change made outside the admin panel), the admin panel shows "Changed from the prep book" at the top with Keep these changes and Undo them; nothing takes effect until the head chef keeps it. If the list changes on another phone while the People tab or Manage staff is open, a draft with nothing changed follows it, and a draft with changes says so and saves only what it changed on top of the latest list, so nobody's save is wiped by a copy taken when the page opened.
- **Head chef (your own name on the list):** set your own name's role to **Head chef** in the admin People tab and save. The People tab says so while no one has it. That name always has every permission and everything a sous chef has (Manage staff, Minutes register, other days, shift pings) on any device with your PIN, and is in `sousIds` and `pingIds`. Only the verified admin can give the role: the save writes `config/main.headId`, which the rules let only the head chef write. Every phone reads the person in `headId` back as Head chef, with every permission and on the list, whatever the people list says (`headFix()` in `js/prep.js`), so a sous chef's save can neither demote nor remove you, and the sous chef's staff page shows "Head chef" in place of Remove. There is only one: picking Head chef for someone else makes the old one Sous chef. What still needs the verified admin session and matching owner grant: the admin panel, writing minutes, saving guides and reading kitchen mail (unless the head chef switched on mail for sous chefs, see Kitchen mail).
- **Pings:** only the head chef and sous chefs write shift pings (20 Oct 2026). Everyone else only reads them. The old Pings tick in the People tab is gone (see Shift pings).
- **New pages (holding pages back from staff):** under New pages in the admin People tab the head chef can untick **Staff see Guides**, **Staff see Shift pings** and **Staff see Meeting minutes** and save. An unticked page is hidden from Staff and View only: no Guides docket, Guides tab or guide links on recipe cards and in the search, no Pings bell or sheet (with both pings and minutes held back), no minutes to sign and no Meeting minutes in the chef mark menu. Opening `#/guides` or `#/minutes` directly says the page is not open to everyone yet. The head chef, sous chefs and leads always see all three. Nothing saved yet means everyone sees everything. It is kept in `config/main` (`staffSee`) and only hides links and skips reads, it is not a lock. Each phone remembers what its person may see (`lk.feat`), so the landing page is right before the book connects.
- **Reads kept small.** Learned prep suggestions are read once a day per phone. Admin reports keep past-day records only in the current page's memory. Returning to reports without reloading reuses that data. A full reload loses Firestore's memory-only copy, so non-empty past days are read from the server again. The `lk.st.got` marker keeps counts and correction times, not report records. Since 2 November 2026 the browser stand-in models that lifetime while retaining its synthetic server fixtures. With 300 learned items and one week of history, the current fixture measures 663 reads for first staff open, 283 with nightly summaries, 24 for staff reload, 633 for first admin open, 0 for returning to already loaded admin reports, and 219 for admin after a full reload. These are representative synthetic samples; startup listeners and test scheduling can change the totals. The budgets below remain the regression limits. The budgets are respectively 680, 300, 40, 760, 60 and 240. The former 60-read admin-reload ceiling relied on the stand-in keeping its cache across reloads. The 240 ceiling records the existing 219-read source behaviour, including refetching 140 past prep and 35 order records; this test correction adds no production reads, polling or persistent private reports. `tests/memory-cache.test.js` checks document, query and known-missing cache lifetime, unchanged backend records and failed-read handling. `tests/read-budget.test.js` also checks that report records remain complete and same-page returns add no past-day server reads. The rollover reads only today and the last written day from the server; the past days on the phone say which day that is (it read all seven before). Kept as they are, with the reason: minutes `loadMine` and mail `mlStatLoad` read one record each because the rules let a person read only their own sign-off or mail status by id (a list query would be refused), and there are only a few; Everything to order (`loadOrdAll`) reads the server when it opens, so two phones never both order the same line; the owner's `activity` listener is one record per person; the 14-day loops stay one query per day, because Firestore bills per record, so one query over 14 days would save only the empty days. **A change to a past day reaches every phone (22 Oct 2026):** when the head chef or a sous chef ticks, unticks, removes or puts back a line on a past prep day, or removes or puts back a past order line, the book stamps `config/main.pastEdit` (`p` prep, `o` orders) with the time (`markPast()`). Every phone already listens to `config/main`, so this costs no new reads: a phone whose saved past days (`lk.past`, `lk.ord.hist`, the admin's `lk.st.got`) are older than the stamp reads them again once (prep: up to 14 days of lines). The rules let only the head chef and sous chefs stamp it, and nothing else in the same write. `tests/past-edits.test.js`. Past days are read once a day per phone and kept on the phone: prep (`lk.past`), order history (`lk.ord.hist`), the wastage summary (`lk.w.sum`, one read of `summary/waste`, see Wastage log), the transfer summary (`lk.x.sum`, one read of `summary/transfers`, see Transfers between venues) and the waste review note (`lk.w.note`). Everything to order reads the last 14 days once (at most once a minute) and then keeps itself in step: today's lines come from the live list and a tick or remove on an older line is applied on the phone. A once-a-day job that fails (the rollover, the order rollover, the written-ahead repair, a sign-off check) waits 5 minutes before trying again, so a phone with no signal or a refused write does not read 8 days of lines on every redraw. A past day that cannot be read (20 Oct 2026) is never kept as an empty day: the order history is only cached when every day came in, otherwise the book tries again after 5 minutes, and Everything to order says "Could not load every day. Check the signal" (the missing day reads "could not load", not "nothing logged") instead of an all-clear. The prep list's past days, the waste review note and the admin Insights and Team days do the same since 28 Oct 2026 (see Opened with no signal). `tests/failed-reads.test.js`.
- **Rollover:** the first person to open today's book writes every line that was not ticked on the last written day (up to 7 days back) onto today, marked "Rolled" (with "x2" and so on for repeats) and sorted to the top of its section. Each rolled line stores `rolled`, `root`, `from` and `rolledFrom`, so the Insights page can show how often and which lines roll over, plus `origBy` and `origAt`: who first wrote it and when (`origAt` is 0 on lines that rolled before October 2026, when it was not kept yet). The line shows who first wrote it and when: "from Ann, yesterday", "from Ann, Mon" within the week, "from Ann, 12 Oct" before that (22 Oct 2026: before, it said only "from Ann", which read as if Ann had been on yesterday). A line that rolled before `origAt` was kept shows only the name, unless it rolled once. Start here counts rolls the same way the line does (a line written for tomorrow is not a roll). There is no Yesterday page any more. A day that holds only a function's platter lines is not a written day, so the open lines from the day before still roll (22 Oct 2026; before, they were left behind). Each phone rolls once per kitchen day, kept in `lk.prep.rolled`, so a reload reads nothing again; a long list goes in batches of 400, so nothing past the first 400 is dropped. `tests/rollover-fn-day.test.js`.
- **Written for tomorrow (22 Oct 2026: by when the list is written).** Every night the team writes the next day's prep list in one go. The book clocks when that batch started: from 15:00, lines written with gaps of 15 minutes or less are one batch, and the biggest batch of 6 or more lines is the night list (a tie goes to the later one; rolled copies, a function's platters, practice and removed lines do not count). `listStart()` in `js/learn.js`, the same code on the phones and on the server. A line written before the batch started is a leftover: it rolls over and shows Rolled. A line written from then on is meant for the next day: when it moves onto that day it looks like a normal line (no tag, the writer's name as usual), and it does not count in Rolled, Stuck, Start here, the CSV or the Insights rolled numbers. Only if it is still open after that day does it show as Rolled (once). The rollover clocks the batch from the night's own lines, which it reads from the server anyway; a night with no batch goes by the time the server clocked for it, then the usual time for that weekday, then for any night (`summary/learned` lists, see Nightly learning), then the usual time of the last tick (the past 14 days), then 20:00 (`cutFor()` in `js/prep.js`). The order list goes by the same time (head chef, 22 Oct 2026): a late order line waits "Since" the next day. Both rollovers wait for the learned list to load (or fail), like they wait for the past days. The admin panel's Reports, Learning page shows "When tomorrow's list is written": the usual start, each weekday (from 2 nights of it) and the last nights with how many lines were in the list. The three numbers (from 15:00, 15 minutes, 6 lines) are at the top of `js/learn.js`, to tune once the head chef has seen the clocked nights. Data: `ahead: true` on the copy (kept as it rolls on); `rolled` still counts the copies, so learning and timings never count a line twice. New copies also keep `origAt` (when the original was written). Lines that rolled over before this rule (or on a phone with older code) are repaired: the first phone to open today's list checks each line that rolled once against its original in the cached past days, and marks it `ahead` for everyone if it was written after the cut. `tests/night-list.test.js`, `tests/written-late.test.js`.
- **Urgent:** the ! button on a line pins it above the rest.
- **Double ups merge (October 2026).** The same item open twice on today's list, in any section, is one job. Lines are the same item when their words match, ignoring case, spacing, punctuation and plurals ("Slice onions" and "slice onion."); amounts agree when they are the same ("2 L" and "2l") or one is blank. Writing an item that is already open (typed, Add from the recipes or Quick add) adds nothing: the open line takes the amount if it had none and the section written, and the toast says "On the list: Pick coriander, 1 bunch." with Add anyway. Double ups already on the list (a line rolled over and written again, two phones at once, older lists) merge by themselves a moment after the list opens: the line written first stays (a rolled line before a new one) and takes the amount, the urgent mark, a section and a recipe link if it had none; the others are removed with `mergedInto`, and the toast has Undo. The line kept shows "Also Fryer" for the other sections it was written under and shows on their tabs too (`also`). The same item with different amounts is not merged: each line shows "Also Prep 5 kg". A ticked line is never a double up (a second batch is a new job). Add anyway, Undo and Put back keep a line apart for good (`dupOk`). Before merging, the phone waits a moment at random (so usually one phone does it) and reads just those lines from the server (one `getDocsFromServer` query by id per 30 lines, a few reads, only when there is a double up), so a phone with an old copy of the list never removes a line ticked somewhere else. Each group is tried once per page load. Merged lines do not count as "Often removed" in Insights. In the code: `dupKey`, `mergeDups`, `openTwin` in `js/prep.js`; tests in `tests/double-ups.test.js`.
- **One item, one amount (22 Oct 2026).** "Slice onion" and "Slice onions" are learned as two records (the record id is the typed words), but the book shows them as one item: counts, weekdays, sections and usual amounts added up, the most used spelling as the name (`foldLearned()` in `js/prep.js`, the same rule as double ups). Hide hides every record of the item. Usual amounts are grouped by the amount as read (`js/amounts.js`), so "2 tubs" and "2tubs" are one chip. A new prep or order line keeps the amount as typed and also saves it read as a number and a unit (`qn`, `qu`) when it can be read for sure, so amounts can be added up later; anything else ("a handful", "2-3 kg") is saved as typed only. Nothing already saved changes. `tests/amounts-book.test.js`.
- **Start here:** with 4 or more open lines today, a card at the top of the list picks up to 3 to do first: urgent lines, then the longest rolled-over, then lines that usually take 2 hours or more (last 7 days), then the ones waiting longest. Tapping one jumps to it. On phones and tablets the card sits above the lines (`.nb-start-l`); on screens 900 px and wider it sits at the top of the Handy page (`.nb-start-r`) so it does not push the lines down. Only one of the two shows.
- **More lines on big screens (900 px and wider):** the notebook page uses the screen height (the old 780 px cap is gone), the left page is wider than Handy (3 to 2), the add row is one line (item, section, amount, Add), the empty hint line is hidden, and with a mouse (`hover: hover`, `pointer: fine`) lines are 40 px high and section headings 32 px. Phones and touch tablets keep the large rows. On short screens (860 px high or less) the dark top bar is 96 px instead of 132 px. At 1920x1080 about 11 lines show where 5 showed before (mock data with six sections).
- **Usual amounts:** while typing a line, the amounts written for it before show as chips ("Usual amount: 2 tubs"). One-tap adds carry the usual amount when one clearly wins. Amounts are kept in `learned/<item>.qtys`.
- **Waste review note:** the latest "change for the next shift" from the last 3 days of waste reviews shows at the top of today's list: above the lines on phones and tablets (`.nb-lc`), at the top of the Handy page on screens 900 px and wider (`.nb-rc`, 23 Oct 2026), beside the lines, so it does not push them down. Only one of the two shows. `tests/closing-waste.test.js`.
- **Ticked lines leave the list.** Once a line is ticked it disappears from the prep list and shows only under Done today in Handy, so the list stays short. On a past day (head chef and sous chefs can browse) the box is headed Done on that day, and the add box and Quick add are not shown: lines are added on today only, because a line written onto a past day would never roll over. A section with nothing left to do is hidden. When everything is ticked the page says so. Ticking shows a short "Ticked ... Undo" message for mis-taps. The same rules apply as before (the ticker for 2 minutes, head chef and sous chefs any time), now through the Undo button in Done today. The header count ("2 of 4 done") and the section counts still include ticked lines. Nothing in the book prints or shares (head chef decision, 20 Oct 2026).
- **Add from the recipes.** The button opens a panel that floats over the list, so the list and the add box do not move or shrink. Nothing is listed until you type a name or tap Show all. The list inside scrolls. Escape, Close or adding a recipe closes it.
- **Order list: lines roll over until ordered.** Like the prep list, anything not ticked as ordered on the last written day is copied onto today's order list by itself, every day, until someone ticks it as ordered (removed lines do not roll). A rolled line shows "Since" and the day it was first written, and "from" the person who wrote it. Each phone tries once a day (`lk.ord.rolled`), the copy's id comes from the first line (`r<date>-<root>`), so two phones never make two copies. Everything to order, the regulars chips and the admin order stats count each rolled item once (its newest copy). Everything to order names a rolled line the way By day does: who first wrote it and the day it was first written ("2 bags (Ann, Wed, 21 Oct)"), not "Rolled over" and today (23 Oct 2026; a very old copy with no `origBy` still says Rolled over). Data: `rolled` (times rolled), `root`, `from`, `rolledFrom`, `since`, `origBy`, `origAt` (when it was first written; the Team tab's time from written to ordered counts from it).
- **Order list: waiting more than 3 days.** On today's list, lines not ordered more than 3 days after they were first written sit together at the top under "Waiting more than 3 days", grouped by the day they were written ("Written Wed 15 Oct, 4 days ago"). A line under the heading says what to do (head chef and sous chefs: order them today or remove what is not needed; staff: tell the head chef or a sous chef). The rows leave out their own "Since" badge there, because the day heading says it. The separate "Still not ordered" card, with Show them and Hide for today, is gone (23 Oct 2026): the block it pointed to is the first thing under the add box anyway. A leftover `lk.ord.nudge` on a phone is never read. `tests/order-learning.test.js`.
- **Order list: it learns.** Every order line written is counted by the nightly learning in `ordlearned/<item>` (`text`, `uses`, `dow` weekday counts, `qtys` usual amounts, `lastUsed`, `lastBy`, `hidden`). The nightly learning counts each finished day (see Nightly learning); the phone writes nothing, it only keeps its own copy in step so the next suggestion already knows. The list is read once a day per phone (`lk.ord.learned`, the 300 most used). It drives the suggestions while typing (most used first, with the usual amount), a Usual amount chip once the name is typed, the "Usually ordered" chips (items ordered 3 or more times on this weekday), and the proofreader's word list. If the list cannot be read (no signal), the order list suggests from the last 14 days only.
- **Order list: the usual amount fills in (22 Oct 2026).** Typing an item the order list knows in full fills the amount box with its usual amount when that is clear (the same amount at least twice, and half the times or more), and the hint under the box says "Usual amount filled in: 6 rolls". The box is selected on tap, so typing replaces it. A name that no longer matches takes the filled amount away; an amount someone typed is never changed. `oUsual()`, `oAutoQty()` in `js/prep.js`; `tests/order-learning.test.js`.
- **Order list: ordered lines move to the bottom.** On today's order list a line stays in the list for 10 minutes after it is ticked as ordered (so a mis-tap is easy to undo), then it leaves the list and shows under "Ordered today" at the bottom, with the time, who ordered it and an Undo button for the head chef and sous chefs. Every line is in one place only. The list belongs to the day, so it is empty again tomorrow; the By day view still shows any past day in full, and "Everything to order" only ever shows lines not yet ordered. The "2 of 3 ordered" count includes the moved lines.
- **Order list layout.** The "What do we need to order?" box is at the top of the page so it is always in reach. On wide screens (1000 px and up) the page is wider: the list on the left and "Ordered today" on the right, side by side under the add box. On phones everything stacks: add box, list, Ordered today. Everything to order stays a single column.
- **Handy page:** only what the list does not already show. In order: Closing soon and the waste review note (big screens), Start here (big screens), Pace and Stuck lines (head chef and sous chefs), For the functions, the "Done today" checklist (who ticked what and when, with an Undo button for anyone allowed to undo that tick), and Quick add (big screens; on phones and tablets the chips are in the add box, so Handy's subtitle says "Done today" there). The old Tools block is gone (20 Oct 2026): Meeting minutes (chefs open the register from its top bar), the drinks run, Shift pings and Manage staff are in the chef mark menu (see Chef mark menu), and print and share are gone. The old "Do these first" box and "Progress by section" bars are gone: the rows already carry Urgent and Rolled, and each section heading and the header already count what is done.
- **Quick add** (Handy page on screens 900 px and wider, the add box on phones and tablets): one row of one-tap chips, in this order: Due today, Usual for the weekday, then Regulars. A line shows once, in the first group it fits, and never when it is already on the list. Each chip shows the usual amount and a small note: "every 3 days, last Thu" (due), "3 Saturdays" (usual for the weekday), "6x" (regular). One tap adds it with its usual amount and section. On phones it is a single sideways swipe row.
- **Ticked lines stay ticked:** a ticked line stays ticked (it moves to Done today in Handy) and cannot be unticked by staff, except the person who ticked it within 2 minutes (for a mis-tap). The head chef and sous chefs can always undo a tick. The rules enforce this on the server. Each new day starts a fresh list (only unfinished lines roll over), so old ticks are never in the way.
- **Weekly badges:** the top 3 ranks for ticked lines over the last 7 days show above the notebook ("Top tickers, last 7 days"; at the bottom of the page on a phone). The person signed in who is not in the top 3 sees their own place at the end of the strip ("You: 5th, 4 ticks"); it was on the "Writing as" line until 23 Oct 2026. The owner can switch them off.
- **Recipe links:** hand-written lines link to recipe cards by matching words against card names and short names (`matchRecipe` in `js/prep.js`). A line that could mean several cards links to none.

- **Contrast and tap targets (20 Oct 2026).** The faint grey is #6B7077 (4.9:1 on the page, was 3.1:1). Admin name fields, role menus, the minutes ticks, suggestion chips and section heads are 44 px high, and admin ticks are 28 px. `tests/targets.test.js`.

### Undo and second taps (October 2026)

- **Small toasts (21 Oct 2026).** Every toast is one pill, 48 to 50 px tall on one line, with Undo or OK still a full 44 px button (it used to be 68 px for "Ticked" and up to four lines, 117 px, for a refused save). The words are kept short enough for two lines on a phone and cut at three; the full message is still read out (`#lk-live`). A swipe down or a tap on the words puts a toast away early, without undoing anything; a toast without a button goes once the page is scrolled (the page moving to the box the toast is about, in its first moment, does not count). Taps on a toast still stop at the toast: nothing behind it is tapped by mistake. Off the prep pages (the landing page, the recipes) a toast without a button shows by itself for its usual time and is not kept for later (23 Oct 2026; "You are on Grill today." after the section pick, "Signed out" from the chef hat); one with Undo or OK still waits for the prep page (`plainToast()`). An A4 page like the iCombi quick guide comes out at about 260 kB. The words on each PDF page are read at the same time for the search. A small 240 px picture of page 1 is kept for the list.
- **Data.** `kitchens/<pin>/guides/<id>`: `title`, `cat`, `who` (`all`, `chefs` or `fn`; missing is `all`), `sum`, `steps` (one per line), `kw` (search words), `text` (words read from the PDF, up to 6000 characters), `thumb`, `pages`, `dims` [{w, h}], `v` (goes up when the file is replaced), `file` (its name), `removed`, `at`, `upd`, `by`. `guides/<id>/pages/<n>`: `img` (a data URL), `w`, `h`, `v`, `n`. Saving writes the pages first, one record each, then deletes pages a shorter new file no longer needs, then the guide, so a guide never shows before its pages. Guides are never deleted, only flagged `removed`. Keep prices, PINs and staff names out of guides.
- **Rules.** Authorised kitchen sessions read guides and their pages under the current access contract. Only the verified admin creates or changes them, with size checks (title 80, summary 200, steps 3000, search words 200, PDF words 8000, small picture 60 kB, page picture 1,000,000 characters, 20 pages). Only the head chef may delete a page picture; a guide itself cannot be deleted.
- **Reads stay small.** The list is read once a day per phone (in the background on any page) and kept on the phone (`lk.guides`), so the recipe cards and the recipe search link to guides with no reads, even with no signal. Opening the Guides page reads the list again when it is older than 30 minutes, so a new guide reaches staff the same shift. A guide's pages are read the first time that version is opened on a phone (one read per page) and then come from the phone's own Firestore copy. No listener, no polling.
- The Backup (Tools) includes the guides' words and small pictures, not the page pictures: keep the original files (for example in the Step by Step Guides folder in Google Drive). Move to a new kitchen PIN copies guides with their pages.
- Not built yet: sous chefs adding guides, ticking off a guide's steps, a count of who has opened a guide.

### Proofreader

- Every new written line is tidied (spacing, punctuation, capital first letter) and spell-checked before it is saved, in the browser, no service needed.
- A word is only corrected when it is not already known and exactly one known word is one slip away (two for long words). Known words come from recipe names and ingredients, a kitchen word list in `js/prep.js` (`KITCHEN`), and everything the list has learned, so new dish names stop being touched once they have been written.
- **Ingredient words stay intact (1 Nov 2026).** Brie is a known kitchen word. Exact words in existing ingredient notes and drawing labels are also protected from replacement, including normalised accents and plurals. These extra words do not become new correction targets. "Cut brie" stays "Cut brie"; clear typos such as "carorts" can still become "carrots". Existing saved lines are not rewritten. This uses loaded recipes and adds no reads.
- **Cooking words stay intact (3 Nov 2026, the head chef).** "Sear steaks" was saved as "Sea steaks", and "Vacuum seal" as "Vacuum sea": sear and seal were not known, and sea (from sea salt) was one letter away. `KITCHEN` now holds the common cooking methods and prep words (sear, seal, poach, braise, simmer, deglaze, vacuum, sous vide, rest, mise and more), so they and their plurals and past tenses are never changed, and a slip in one ("Braiss") still becomes the word. Everyday words one letter from a new kitchen word (have, soft, store, sort, probe, scone and others) are in `PLAIN`, which only protects them and never offers them as a correction, so "have" never becomes "halve". Existing saved lines are not rewritten. No reads. `tests/proofreader.test.js`.
- A corrected line keeps what was typed in `orig`. A toast offers Undo, and an undone word is left alone on that device. Reports, Insights still shows the latest corrections and links to the admin Proofreader.
- **Admin controls (1 November 2026).** Review desk, Proofreader has Corrections, Always keep and History. The verified admin can Restore original or Edit wording on prep from the last 60 days and staff orders from the last 30. A repair preserves amounts, status and authorship, clears derived recipe links and records an immutable private event. Always keep protects up to 200 individual words across updated staff devices through the existing config listener, with no new staff reads. It does not rewrite saved lines or existing learned usage counts. Failed and stale saves keep the draft. Back keeps unfinished work behind Resume. See [Admin proofreader](proofreader-admin.md) for access, limits, backup and release details.

- **Sections are spell-checked too.** A section one slip away from a section already in use (two for long names) becomes that section, so "Fryre" goes under "Fryer" instead of starting a new one. Singular and plural are the same section ("Salad" goes under "Salads"). A real word is never swapped for a different section ("Pans" stays "Pans" even when "Pass" exists). Otherwise longer words are checked like a line and the first letter becomes a capital. It happens when you leave the section box (toast with Undo) and again when the line is saved; an undone section is left alone on that device (`lk.prep.keepsec`). The wastage log's section box works the same way.

### Nightly backup (on the server)

- Every night at 03:30 kitchen time a GitHub Action (`.github/workflows/nightly-backup.yml`) runs `scripts/nightly-backup.mjs` (19 Oct 2026). It reads the book the way the admin panel's Backup button does and writes one JSON file in the same shape: people, prep lines, order lines, wastage and end-of-day reviews and transfers between venues from the last 45 days, every shift ping with who has seen it, every meeting minutes with earlier versions and sign-offs, who worked which kitchen section, what the prep and order lists have learned, the learning log, tallies, sections, empty searches, card opens, the guides' words and small pictures, the head chef's recipe changes with their earlier versions, shared protected words and every private proofreader event, and who has set a PIN. - The file is encrypted on the runner with authenticated AES-256-GCM and the existing `BACKUP_KEY` secret, using Node's built-in crypto and PBKDF2-SHA256. The repository is public and anyone signed in to GitHub can download an artifact, so only encrypted output is uploaded and an always-run step removes both plaintext files. Authentication and JSON checks finish before the private `0600` output appears; existing destinations are refused. Older OpenSSL archives use explicit `decrypt-legacy` mode, which cannot verify their authenticity. [Commands, included data, omissions and recovery limits](backup-recovery.md).

### Changing the kitchen PIN

1. Add a second copy of the `kitchens/<pin>/...` rules for the new 4 digit PIN to the live rules (see `firestore.rules`).
2. Owner panel, "Move to a new 4 digit kitchen PIN" (head chef only: the admin panel requires the matching head-chef PIN and owner device grant, the code checks again, and the new PIN only works once its rules are published in the Firebase console; a second tap confirms): copies the people, PIN fingerprints (each person's `pinset` record first; with the rules from 19 Oct 2026 the head chef may copy a fingerprint as it is, so a stored PIN for someone no longer on the list does not stop the move), prep lines, order lines, wastage and reviews, transfers between venues (400 days), learned items, empty searches, tallies, shift pings and their seen records, minutes with their earlier versions and sign-offs, what the order list has learned, guides with their page pictures, kitchen mail, drinks, kitchen sections and the chef to-do list to the new PIN, then switches that device over. Copying sign-offs and seen records needs the rules from 19 Oct 2026 (the head chef may create them); with older rules they stay on the old PIN and the message says how many.
3. Everyone else is asked for the PIN next time they open the book once the old rules are removed. Remove the old PIN's rules last, so nothing is cut off while moving.
4. "Use a different kitchen PIN on this device" (same block, two taps) makes the head chef's own device forget its kitchen PIN and go to the PIN screen of the prep book, for example to look at the old PIN again. 4. Change the `KITCHEN_PIN` secret in the GitHub repository settings to the new PIN, or the nightly learning keeps counting the old PIN's (now empty) days and nothing new is learned.

`scripts/publish-rules.sh` fills in one PIN. For step 1 (two PINs live at once) and step 3, paste a hand-edited copy into the Firebase console instead, as described in the header of `firestore.rules`.
