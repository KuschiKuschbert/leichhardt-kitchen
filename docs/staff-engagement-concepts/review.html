Three additional insights

01

Handover changes

Find which items and sections keep being added after the agreed prep handover. Use the pattern to improve tomorrow's list.

Existing creation times and section/item links; needs an agreed handover time. Recorded list changes do not measure work speed.
02

Concerns followed through

See which equipment, safety and workflow reports still need attention, with their age and current status.

Existing Kitchen mail records. Marked done and opening the inbox do not prove the underlying problem was fixed.
03

Attribution and setup help

Check usable person references on records, and ask who wants help getting their own app ready.

Existing person IDs plus an optional confirmed setup status. A saved name or installed app does not prove who acted or who owns a phone.

Three ways to encourage useful waste reporting

01

A useful-record receipt

Quietly acknowledge a successfully saved item, amount, unit and reason under the person's name.

Recognise the habit once. More entries or more kilograms earn no extra score.
02

A calm section check

Offer Log something to add or Checked; nothing to add at a natural stopping point.

Proposed new self-declaration. It is not zero waste, a whole-kitchen guarantee or the chef's review.
03

Learning carried forward

Show the chef's resulting prep follow-up so staff can see their reporting leads somewhere useful.

Builds on the existing review-to-prep flow. No unmeasured claim of waste or money saved.

Three ways to make own-name use worthwhile

01

Your name, your contribution

Keep the current name clear, offer Change person nearby and show Saved as your name.

A valid personal PIN on the shared screen counts just as much as one on a phone.
02

Pocket book ready

Optional help to add the app to the home screen and sign in, with a small setup mark and Not now.

Convenience, with no pressure to use a personal phone and no background ownership tracking.
03

My app-day card

Bring the person's chosen section, relevant pings and next useful task together. Keep any habit history private.

App sign-ins and app days are not attendance or payroll clock-ins. A private history would need a new self-only summary.

A small first step

Start with the Shift Card.

Pair clear own-name attribution with useful save feedback. Decide on explicit section checks and private history separately before adding their data fields.
