# The mascot on wide screens: three designs and a mix

11 November 2026. Review-only previews on the real book (main at 925d985, stand-in data, made-up people, real fonts). Nothing in the book has changed. Round 1 said Build smaller, 5 of 5: see [the brief](brief.md) and [the evidence packet](evidence-packet.md). The preview's source is in `preview/`; the pictures are in `evidence/`.

## The task walked through every design

A cook at the Pans screen (1920 x 1080, on the Pans tab) ticks an ordinary line, then the last Pans line. Another screen ticks a Grill line. Another screen writes a new Pans line, and the cook ticks it. Then, on the All list, the rest of the kitchen's lines are ticked, one of which finishes a section while lines are left, and the last of which finishes the list. Then Home.

Stand-in list: 9 lines in four sections (Grill 2, Pans 2, Fryers 2, Hot larder 3), one removed line, the Top tickers strip showing, the Lunch team set.

## What is the same in A, B, C and the mix

- Taps to tick a line: 1 before, 1 after (2 on a shared screen, the tick then the name, as today). No new control, sheet, setting, sound, read, write or rules line.
- While a line is left on the section's own tab, the book is today's book, to the pixel.
- The slip is untouched at every width: "Ticked Caramelise onions", Undo, and the sentence "Pans are done. Grill has 2 left." exactly as built (#673).
- The words are the built words. No new sentence is written for wide screens.
- Nothing under 900 px wide changes. Measured at 768 x 1024 and 390 x 844: every rectangle is where it is today.
- Size is chosen by measuring, not by width alone: the largest of full and half that adds no scroll to the list page; if neither fits, today's own words stay.

## A. Words first

**Central idea.** The gap round 1 found is reading distance, so fix the words and add no art. The cheapest baseline: it shows what the larger words do alone.

- **A finished section on its own tab** (new). Where the page says "Nothing left to do in Pans. Ticked lines are under Done today in Handy." it says, in the book's done-green speech shape, "Pans are done." and under it "Grill has 2 left.", both at 40 px bold (28 px at half size). The page's pointer stays under it: "See Done today under Handy." No mascot.
- **The finished list on All.** The card that is live says "Every line ticked. 9 of 9." at 40 px. The mascot keeps the 64 x 85 px he has, under the words.
- It is a state, like the card: 2 lines or more, every one ticked and on the server, nothing waiting to be sent, the same silences as the slip sentence. It follows the list on every draw: another screen's tick changes "2 left" to "1 left"; a new line takes it away; it comes back when that line is ticked. When no next section can be named it says only "Pans are done."

Pros: answers the reading gap with type alone; nothing to load, so nothing to fail; the least to learn and the least to test; no picture that can be read before the words.
Cons: on a section's own tab the mascot still never shows, which is the screen the owner asked about; it reads as a status line, not a cheer; it changes the same approved rule as B (a section's own tab keeps its plain words) for less.

## B. The finished page is his

**Central idea.** At a finished state the list page is empty anyway, so that is where he gets his drawn size, under his words. Rare, in the page, and gone the moment a line is open.

- Everything in A, and he stands under the words: 192 x 256 px (his full drawn size) where the measured room holds it, from 1280 px wide; 96 x 128 px (half) from 900 px wide or where the room is shorter; today's book where neither fits.
- **On a section's own tab he is a still** (the Welcome pose). Not a control: a tap does nothing.
- **On the All list** the card is the same scene at the same sizes, and keeps the one Serve play it has today on the screen whose tick finished the list.
- For up to 9 seconds after the finishing tick the sentence is on screen twice: in the slip (16 px, with Undo) and in the page (large). After that only the page holds it.

Pros: the only design that puts the mascot on a section's own screen at a size seen from the bench, without anything beside a live list; one pattern for both finished states (the Finished-list card pattern, extended); a state, so Undo, another screen, a reload, midnight and 04:00 correct it with no flag.
Cons: changes an approved rule (a section's own tab keeps its plain words); the largest picture on the screen can be read before the words ("Pans are done" taken as "prep is done"); words twice for 9 seconds; on a shared screen it is seen for at most 10 minutes after the last touch; a screen kept on the All list sees it only when the whole list is finished.

## C. He comes to the finish, beside the page

**Central idea.** The page is left exactly as it is today. He has a place outside it, in the empty gutter to the right of the book, and he is there only while there is a finish to say. This is the skeptic's contrast from round 1, and the only design that says a finished section large on a screen kept on the All list.

- **Where.** A fixed place in the right gutter, from 1600 px wide (the page stops at 1240 px, so the gutter is 340 px at 1920, 100 px at 1440, 20 px at 1280). 192 x 256 px from 1800 px wide, 96 x 128 px from 1600 px. Below 1600 px the design shows nothing new.
- **When.** (1) Any tick that finishes a section, on the All list or its own tab: he appears with the slip's own sentence in a large bubble (32 px) and leaves when the slip leaves, 9 seconds at most. On the All list that is while other lines are left. (2) While the view in front of the cook is finished (the section on its own tab, the list on All) he stays, with the same words, as a state.
- A still. The finished list keeps its one Serve play, played from his own place. One mascot at a time: the card's small still gives way to him.
- Not a resident: while there is nothing to say he is not on the screen.

Pros: no approved page rule changes on the tab (its plain words stay); the only design that reaches a screen kept on the All list at a finished section; one place to look on every Prep view.
Cons: it shows something beside a list while lines are left, which four round 1 reviewers ruled out; it appears and disappears in the corner of the eye at the moment the cook reaches for the next tick; it exists only from 1600 px wide, so a zoomed 1920 screen (1280 px) gets nothing; it is a layer outside the page, with its own questions under the Who did this? sheet, the PIN screen and at a width no click test opens; the words sit in two places 1,000 px apart for 9 seconds.

## The mix: B in the page, and C's arrival for the All list

One coherent preview (`M` in the preview's source). Everything in B. And, from 1600 px wide only, when a tick finishes a section **on the All list**, he appears in the gutter with the slip's sentence for the slip's 9 seconds, then leaves. On a section's own tab and at the finished list, B's scene in the page speaks and the gutter stays empty.

It is here because A and B do nothing for a screen kept on the All list until the whole list is finished, and which page the five screens keep open is not known.

How it was measured: each design was switched on over the real book and the same walk was run; every rectangle a cook works with (list rows, section headers, tabs, the list and Handy page headers, the count, Add a line, the top bar, every button and link in Handy, the page foot and its Show removed link, the slip and its Undo, every Home card) was recorded at each step and compared with today's book at the same step. "Moved" counts rectangles that differ. The scene, the card and the mascot himself are left out of that count: they are the change.

Also measured:
- **It follows the list** (B, 1920 x 1080): after another screen ticked a Grill line the page said "Grill has 1 left."; after another screen wrote a Pans line the scene was gone and the line was there; when that line was ticked the scene was back, while the slip stayed plain (the slip says a section once a day).
- **A zoomed screen** (1280 x 720, a 1920 screen at 150%): B chose half size, 96 x 128 px with 28 px words, and added no scroll.
- **Extra large text** (1920 x 1080 and 1280 x 720): no rectangle moved and no scroll was added; the size chosen is in the table.
- **A sous chef** (the page foot shows "Show removed (1)"): the foot and its link did not move.
- **Colour**: the large words are the book's done ink (#237347) on the done tint (#EAF5EE), the pair the live card and slip sentence use. Worked out from the two colours: 5.2 to 1 (4.5 to 1 is the mark for small text, 3 to 1 for large). Not measured on a screen.
- Under 900 px the preview is switched off: its styles sit inside a 900 px rule and its script stops before it draws. At 768 x 1024 and 390 x 844 every rectangle is where it is today, and pictures of a design differ from today's book only where two runs of today's book also differ (the slip's moving timer line, a line caught mid-animation, Home's greeting words).

**A shared kitchen screen** (1920 x 1080, [`7-shared.png`](evidence/7-shared.png)): with B nothing of the design is on screen while Who did this? is up, and the scene stands on the finished tab after the name is chosen. With C and the mix, when the cook goes straight to the next tick after a section is finished on the All list, he is still in the gutter behind the Who did this? sheet: dimmed and blurred with the rest of the page, not sharp beside it. The idle lock and the PIN screen were not exercised with a design switched on.

Not measured, so not evidenced: how any of it reads from 1.5 m on a real section screen; reduced motion and a screen reader with a design switched on; a real touch screen; a list of real length (the stand-in has 9 lines); midnight and the 04:00 refresh with a scene standing; whether any of it changes how much prep is done.

## Questions the designs leave for the council

1. **A play on a section's own tab?** As drawn he is a still there. One play (Flip) on the screen whose own tick finished the section, once per section per kitchen day per device on a guard that survives the lock, is the alternative.
2. **Full size or half?** 192 x 256 px with 40 px words where the room holds it, or stop at 96 x 128 px with 28 px words everywhere.
3. **Words twice for up to 9 seconds** (slip and page) in B: acceptable, or a fault?
4. **Home.** Drawn apart for the owner ([`6-apart.png`](evidence/6-apart.png)): under the cards at 192 x 256 px (1920 x 1080) or 96 x 128 px (1280 x 900), where no card moves. Round 1 cut a bigger Home mascot; it would also change the owner's choice of 9 November (the heading corner).

## After round 2: the blend

Round 2 ranked B first for four of six and C last for all six, and four reviewers asked for the same blend of A and B ([the brief](brief.md)). It is `P` in the preview's source: B's scene in the page with A's order of sizes.

- The words are sized first, one size for both finished states on a screen: 40 px from 1280 px wide where the room in view above the slip holds them, 28 px otherwise, today's book if neither fits.
- Then the largest mascot that still fits, by measuring, then none. On a finished section's own tab he is 96 x 128 px at most, never taller than his words. His drawn size, 192 x 256 px, is kept for the whole finished list (then 96 x 128, then 64 x 85, then words alone).
- The page's pointer keeps the book's own size, under the scene. What is left is drawn in the page's ink, what is done in the done green ([`8-blend-1920.png`](evidence/8-blend-1920.png) shows both ways).
- Repairs from round 2, all in the preview: lines left and no section to name, today's plain words stay; the words never wait for his file or his art; with no signal, or anything not sent, today's plain words stay.
