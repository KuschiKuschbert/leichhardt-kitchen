Shared ground rules

The pixel art and navy bandana stay intact. The existing app type and paper surfaces stay. No mascot appears over a service list, replaces a plating drawing, or signals Saved, food safety or a running cook.

Reduced motion starts still. Playback pauses when hidden or offscreen. Artwork loads only where it is used; missing artwork must leave kitchen tasks usable. The full collection does not need to ship in the app's startup cache.
