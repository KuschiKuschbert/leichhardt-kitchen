# Design and behaviour patterns

The pieces the Kitchen Book is built from, so a new screen looks and behaves like the rest (head chef, 8 Nov 2026: "maintain consistency"). Before drawing anything new, use one of these. If nothing fits, add the new pattern here in the same pull request, with its class and helper names, and get the head chef's OK for a new look (the service council, `.claude/skills/service-council/SKILL.md`).

Every class, helper and colour named on this page is also listed in `tests/design-patterns.test.js`, which fails when one of them is gone from the code (a test may not read `docs/`, see `CLAUDE.md`). Change the two together. The same test fails when a file declares one top-level name twice.

The rules behind them are in `CLAUDE.md` (Service rules, Coding rules, Things that are easy to break). The longer notes: [after the tap](action-feedback.md), [adaptive controls](adaptive-controls.md), [loading and motion](loading-motion.md), [work pages](work-pages-design-options/brief.md), [admin pages](admin-pages-options/brief.md).

## The look

- **Current break board** (Today's team, 9 November 2026): reuse `.hm-board` on `.tm-away`, with the existing charcoal surface, light text, orange return times, edge and pin. Label it On break now, with Until beside each return time. Keep the notebook position and the saved all-shift names, meal breaks, splits and return times in `.tm-away-slot`. `tmAwayHidden()` hides the entire board only for a checked current saved plan with no active break or split and no invalid times. Keep its DOM for clock paints to show it again, and keep unknown/error warnings visible. The existing status renderer and clock paints remain authoritative. [Contract and checks](team-notebook-options/brief.md#hide-a-confirmed-empty-board-9-november-2026).

- **Colours** are tokens in `:root` in `css/app.css`, never a new hex in a rule: `--ink` (text, rules), `--paper` and `--bg` (surfaces), `--muted` and `--faint` (quiet text), `--line` (dividers), `--accent` and `--accent-ink` (the book's orange), `--tag` (needs attention: a filled chip, a due line), `--done` and `--done-ink` (saved, ticked), `--bar` and `--bar-text` (the dark top bar and tab bar).
- **Operational colours** (whole-app council, 9 November 2026): retain orange for actions, selected notebook controls and attention, green for completion or explicit assignment, and neutral charcoal/grey for ordinary information. Use `--bad-ink` and `--bad-tint` for failed reads/writes, invalid entries, cancellation and destructive actions. On charcoal use `--bad-on-dark` and `--done-on-dark` so status text stays readable. Colour always accompanies existing words, ticks, pressed states or outlines; an assignment in a draft is not a confirmed save. Preserve the named charcoal Urgent flag. Claimed Prep work keeps full text contrast, with the claimant's neutral badge and name. Food drawings, allergen certainty, wastage reasons, chart scales, seasonal art and approved card surfaces have separate meanings. See [the inventory and council](colour-coding.md).
- **Surface colour** (owner-approved hybrid, 9 November 2026): coloured covers, headers and spines identify purpose while writing areas stay quiet. Use `--surface-work` and `--surface-work-edge` for work, including Orders; `--surface-people` and `--surface-people-edge` for people; `--surface-paper` for interiors; and `--surface-reference` for quiet reference and forms. Current live boards and bands retain charcoal. Home retains its approved purpose surfaces. These surface identities do not imply due, done or Saved. Preserve category, chart, art and print palettes. [Chosen contract, council and exceptions](surface-colours.md).
- **Attention and outcome fills** remain local: the top bar chip until today's team is saved, an attention banner, a filled tag or an acknowledged result. Amber means attention or pending, green confirms the stated success and red marks errors or existing adverse/destructive actions. Retain explicit assignment/inclusion labels on existing green controls. Groups use space and headings first; the approved purpose surfaces are a bounded exception, not a reason to box every group.
- **Notebook colour** (Chef to-do and Today's team, updated 9 November 2026): keep the approved work or people cover and spine, warm writing paper, orange active choices, green ticks and explicitly completed or assigned states, and quiet neutral dividers. Use `--small-ink` for small orange text and selected fills with `--bar-text`; `--done-ink` for completion; `--faint` for readable control outlines. Local attention and scheduled-away cues retain their words. Colour reinforces checkmarks, pressed states and strikethrough. Preserve the approved spacing, target sizes and acknowledgement timing.
- **Type:** `--display` (Barlow Condensed) for headings and labels, `--body` (Barlow) for the rest, Caveat for the handwritten done count (`.nb-prog`).
- **Corners:** `--r-act` (8 px) on buttons that do something (Save, Add), `--r-pick` (round) on chips and filters that choose.
- **Tap targets** are at least 44 px; a quiet secondary link may be smaller when a 44 px way to the same place is on screen.
- **Icons** are inline SVG with `currentColor` and `aria-hidden="true"`, from `ICON` in `js/app.js`; an icon drawn more than once with a mask gets its own mask id each time (`tmIcon()`).

## A page

- **The card:** `section.p-card` with `h2.p-h` (id `rtitle`, so the page's Back and focus land on it), a `.hint` line for what the page is for and when it was read, then the work. Buttons sit in a `.btn-row`: `.btn` for most, `.btn.dark` for the one main action. A quiet action in a sentence is a `.p-link` (Refresh, Try again).
- **A failed read** never shows "nothing", "nobody" or "0": a `.p-err` line with role status says it could not load, a Try again, and the book tries once more after 5 minutes by itself (`retrySoon()`, `waiting()`). An answer from the phone's own copy with no signal is not "none" (`cacheOnly()`).
- **Waiting** uses the Ink helpers, never a spinner: `inkLabel()` in a line, `inkWait()` for a block (`docs/loading-motion.md`).
- **Home's board** (redesign step 2, 8 Nov 2026): a `.hm-board` with only what is still to come; Your day stays charcoal and Now and next uses the existing warm amber surface with dark text and orange labels, one `.hm-a` row each (a drawing, the words with a small second line, the time and a short note on the right, `is-hot` when it is close), every row a tap to where it is done. Under it, `.hm-paper` cards hang from a pin (Prep by section with its `.hm-sec` rows and bars, Coming up). Worked out from what the phone holds, never a read of its own; `homeSlot()` draws it and `homePaint()` keeps it right. A confirmed empty work board is hidden; loading and failed checks retain their own words.
- **Staff-first Home** (9 Nov 2026): `.hm-staff-first` groups the native Your day board (section, shift hours, full break and return) then the soft sage paper card with visible coworkers on the `.hm-rota` board (sections down, shifts across, each `.hm-tp` with its break, 10 Nov 2026), before work reminders and the cool steel Prep by section card. These two existing-palette surfaces and inset edges identify the card purpose; labels and progress states retain their meaning. Scheduled same-shift or overlapping-shift coworkers exclude self; without a personal assignment use On today. The full saved day stays in a native disclosure with `.hm-team-staff` when the card holds only one shift; a card that already holds the whole day has no fold. Its open state survives clock paints. Checking, unset and failed reads have distinct words. It uses the loaded saved team, never the planner draft. Desktop from 1100 px uses matching equal columns for the personal/team and work tiers, full-width quiet Prep and a final upcoming row; smaller views keep their existing flow. [Contract](home-staff-first.md).
- **Named Home heading** (A chosen 10 Nov 2026): the existing `.wall-h` uses `.hm-welcome` for a varied welcome with the **full roster name**, with weekday/date underneath. `homeWelcomeWords()` keeps one phrase per Home visit, current identity, Queensland day and active celebration. Celebration wishes come first, then the person's shift moment (`LK_PREP.homeGreet()`), then ordinary words with Queensland time of day; `lk-greet` re-picks only when the moment or hour changes. It avoids the previous phrase on return, keeps ordinary wording for Off/reflective/civic occasions, and never rerolls on a roster rename or routine repaint. Use the authenticated personal staff name, not the remembered Google account or a guessed first word; public/locked views keep a neutral heading. Long names wrap. Below 768 px, `.hm-welcome-long` uses `1.5rem` above 21 complete heading characters and `.hm-welcome-compact` uses `1.2rem` above 37, retaining the app's text-size preference. Shorter prefixes retain at least the original name-based compact size. Existing identity/config events repaint the greeting and open menu in place, with one Unicode-aware initial helper for the hat, menu and Settings. Named Home leaves anchored seasonal ornaments out of the heading area; the celebration note and background ambience remain. No new read, banner or service control. Validate complete-shell long-name/enlarged-text geometry rather than reusing the historical preview metrics. [Scope and evidence](staff-personalisation-options/brief.md#implementation-10-november-2026).
- **Home mascot** (owner, 9 Nov 2026): `.mascot` is a decorative button absolutely placed in `.mascot-host` (`#main.wall-main`) at the right end of the Welcome row, outside `#rtitle`, standing on the date line, 64 or 48 px wide. When the welcome words fill that corner (owner, 10 Nov 2026, B), it stands 48 x 64 or 44 x 59 px on the Your day card's header line, left of `.hm-clock`, peeking over the card top (`data-spot="card"`); nothing moves. With no Your day card (Functions-only Home), `.mascot-room` on `#rtitle` and `.wall-date` keeps 76 px for it and the words wrap (`data-spot="room"`). It is left out only when none of these clears every word of the name, date and card title, a seasonal ornament and the top bar. One play of the person's daily action, then the Welcome pose; a tap replays it. No hover tint or pressed box; keyboard focus ring only; `scale` press inside `:where()`. Home only. [Feature note](kitchen-mascot.md).
- **Finished-list card** (owner, 10 Nov 2026): when every line of today's own prep list is ticked and the server has every tick, `.nb-fin` takes the place of the page's own "ticked off" sentence in `.nb-body`: `.nb-fin-art` (the mascot's room, 48 x 64 px, 64 x 85 px from 900 px, kept before the art loads so nothing under it moves) and `.nb-fin-w` with `.nb-fin-say`, a speech shape in `--done-tint` and `--done-ink` with no outline ("Every line ticked. 6 of 6."), above the page's own `.nb-empty` pointer to Done today. In the page, never over it, and not a control. The art is `.mascot-still` from `js/mascot.js` (`sprite()`), moved into each redraw by `finPaint()`; `finOk()` is the one test for showing it. A state, not an event: nothing while a line is left or Not sent, and Undo, a new line or another phone correct it. One Serve play on the phone that finished the list, once a kitchen day, then the Welcome pose. See [the mascot note](kitchen-mascot.md#under-a-finished-prep-list).
- **Finished words, wide** (owner, 11 Nov 2026): from 900 px wide the Finished-list card, and a finished section on its own tab, become one scene in the empty list page: `.nb-fin.nb-scene`, the words on top in the same speech shape (`.nb-w40` 40 px, `.nb-w28` 28 px), the mascot under them (`.nb-f192` for the whole list only, `.nb-f96`, `.nb-f64`, `.nb-f48`, or none with `.nb-f0` and `.nb-noart`; on a section's tab never taller than his words), a tail that points down at him and grows with the words, then the page's own `.nb-empty` pointer at the book's size. While a row still shows on the list page (a ticked line lingering, or removed lines a chef asked to see) the book keeps the small card and the plain words. A finished section's scene is `.nb-fin-sec`, built by `finFit()` beside the tab's plain sentence, which stays in the page for a narrow window, no signal or an older stylesheet; it says the slip's own sentence on two lines, both lines in `--done-ink`, like the slip (owner, 11 Nov 2026). Words first: `finFit()` picks one size of words for both states on a screen from the room in view above the slip, then the largest mascot that fits. A state, a still on a section's tab, not a control, and it moves no line, tab or button. See [the mascot note](kitchen-mascot.md#the-finished-words-on-a-wide-screen).
- **Slip sentence** (owner, 10 Nov 2026): the slip of a tick that finished a section carries one more line of words under "Ticked ...": `.p-toast-two` on the slip (a two-row grid: the words, then the sentence, with Undo beside both) and `.p-toast-say` (bold, `--done-ink`, one line, never wrapped). Words only: no art, no control. It appears only once the server has the tick, only on that tick's own slip, and `secFit()` in `js/prep.js` takes it off again when it would not fit on one line or would make the slip taller than the plain slip, so the slip keeps its height and Undo its place. A tap on it does nothing. Nothing else may add a line to a slip without the head chef's OK. [Note](kitchen-mascot.md#a-finished-section-on-the-slip).
- **A long card on a phone** (the recipe card, redesign step 6, 8 Nov 2026) keeps its row of jump chips under the top bar while it scrolls (`.jumps`, sticky below 900 px, scrolling sideways with a faded edge when the parts do not fit). The main parts come first, so they show on a 390 px phone (Allergens, Ingredients, Method), then the drawings (Plate or Pan, Side view). A jump lands just under the row, never behind it.
- **A switch on a trail line** (Keep screen on, head chef 14 Nov 2026): one choosing chip at the right of a recipe's trail line (`.ko-row` around the trail, `.ko-chip`), 44 px, the choosing chip's round corners. Off is the plain outline with its action in words ("Keep screen on"); on is an orange edge and orange words with a tick ("Screen on"), shown only while the thing it names is really true. Both words share one cell, so the chip keeps its width; one accessible name with `aria-pressed`. A tap does what the words said when the finger went down. A refusal is one red line under the row (`.ko-say`, `role="alert"`) until the next tap or leaving. On a phone the trail keeps its links and drops the page's own name, which is the heading right under it (still read out). Only where the device can do it. [Brief](keep-screen-on-options/brief.md).
- **A card's head** puts the name and its number on one line and the descriptive line across the whole card under them (`.card-head.ch-grid`, `.ch-line`), so the line never wraps to a narrow column beside a big number.
- **A search** is one pill with its magnifier inside (`.find-i`) and the word Search for screen readers only. On a phone its count line shows only while a search or filter is on.
- **Words** follow `CLAUDE.md`, Writing style: short, 24-hour times, no em dashes. A person's name comes from the staff list (`mnName()`), never typed into code.

## Choosing

- **Two to about six fixed choices:** chips in a row (`.chip` with `aria-pressed`), one tap. A count on a chip goes in a `small` inside it (Today's team's shift chips).
- **Configured shift tabs with a day review** (Today's team, retained by C Person worksheet chosen 9 Nov 2026): `.tm-viewbar` keeps Whole day visible beside `.tm-shift-scroll`, a row of configured shifts that scrolls within the page and leaves the page itself fitting the screen. Whole day reviews the draft; it is never a shift ID. Choose a real shift, then use each person's own section picker. Name the shift and person in controls. Changing or clearing a section affects that shift only and keeps membership and breaks. Not on the named shift is an explicit removal choice and removes only that shift's breaks. All views keep the same draft and Save.
- **More, or a list from the kitchen's settings:** a `<select>` that the shared controls dress as a picker (`LK_UX.enhance`, `.ux-picker`): a bottom sheet on a phone, a centred box from 768 px.
- **More than about 12:** the picker gets a type-to-find box with the likely few first (`AdaptivePicker` in `js/controls.js`).
- **Fill in what the book knows** and keep it easy to change: the usual section, the unit, last week's team (Use it), a long shift's split. A suggestion fills every field it knows. Nothing is saved by itself.
- **Filled for you** (head chef, 13 Nov 2026, `docs/smart-book-options/brief.md`): when the book fills a box from what it knows (an item typed in full on Wastage or Transfers, the usual order amount), the box carries a word tag inside it (`.lk-fb`, `.lk-ftag`: LAST TIME or USUAL, a neutral word in a box, never green or orange), a label carries the same tag, and one line right under the boxes (`.lk-fillnote`) says what was filled and where from, with a 44 px button to empty it. It fills only empty boxes, never over what was typed or picked, and never a measured amount: a weight or count stays the cook's, the box outlined (`.lk-weigh`) and Log it off with its words beside it (`.lk-why`). A new name takes the filled values away; a filled value is not kept for the next line. Draw with `fbHTML()` and `ftagHTML()`; a fill started by leaving a box waits for the finger to come up (`fillAfterTap()`) and patches in place, so the tap that left the box still lands.
- **Suggestions while typing** sit right under the box as rows (`.nb-sgr`), the likely few first, each saying what the book knows on a small second line. A tap on the words fills the form; a one-tap **Add** beside them (`.nb-sgadd`) only when every field it needs is known, never guessed (Add a line, redesign step 1, 8 Nov 2026).

## Saving and after the tap

- **A form's Save:** `svButton()` draws it (off while nothing changed: `save()` refuses then too), `svStart()` when it goes, `svDone()` only when the server answers, `svFail()` on a refusal, `svLine()` for the line under it. Red failure words come from `errMsg()`; an unknown refusal goes through `failed()`.
- **A tap on a list line** answers on the slip with the line's name and Undo: `toast()`, `slipName()`. Answer the six questions in `docs/action-feedback.md` for every new button.
- **Destroying something typed** asks a second tap in place: `arm()` and `armed()` turn the button into "Tap again to remove". No pop-up. The book has one helper (`js/prep.js`, through `LK_PREP.book()`: `arm(key, redraw)`, `armed(key)`, `unarm()`); a lazy page prefixes its keys (`mg:`, `fe:`) and keeps its own copy only as the fallback for an older book.
- **Every write** goes through `W()`; ask `canW()` first.
