# Design and behaviour patterns

The pieces the Kitchen Book is built from, so a new screen looks and behaves like the rest (head chef, 8 Nov 2026: "maintain consistency"). Before drawing anything new, use one of these. If nothing fits, add the new pattern here in the same pull request, with its class and helper names, and get the head chef's OK for a new look (the service council, `.claude/skills/service-council/SKILL.md`).

Every class, helper and colour named on this page is also listed in `tests/design-patterns.test.js`, which fails when one of them is gone from the code (a test may not read `docs/`, see `CLAUDE.md`). Change the two together. The same test fails when a file declares one top-level name twice.

The rules behind them are in `CLAUDE.md` (Service rules, Coding rules, Things that are easy to break). The longer notes: [after the tap](action-feedback.md), [adaptive controls](adaptive-controls.md), [loading and motion](loading-motion.md), [work pages](work-pages-design-options/brief.md), [admin pages](admin-pages-options/brief.md).

Label it On break now, with Until beside each return time. Keep the notebook position and the saved all-shift names, meal breaks, splits and return times in `.tm-away-slot`. `tmAwayHidden()` hides the entire board only for a checked current saved plan with no active break or split and no invalid times. Keep its DOM for clock paints to show it again, and keep unknown/error warnings visible. The existing status renderer and clock paints remain authoritative. [Contract and checks](team-notebook-options/brief.md#hide-a-confirmed-empty-board-9-november-2026). On a phone the board's names end above the sticky Save dock on the first screen: below 720 px the admin panel's Today's team takes the chef panel's one-line Team heading (`.tm-admin-plan`, 16 Nov 2026, [Board above the dock](team-minimum-options/brief.md#board-above-the-dock-16-november-2026)), and on every phone the board sits at the top of Today's team, above the notebook head and the date (`away` first in `.tm-focus-page .tm-nbook`'s areas, 16 Nov 2026, [Short phones](team-minimum-options/brief.md#short-phones-16-november-2026)); from 720 px it stays under the date. Anything new above the board keeps `tests/team-minimum.test.js` parts 10 and 11 green.

- **Colours** are tokens in `:root` in `css/app.css`, never a new hex in a rule: `--ink` (text, rules), `--wall-ink` (quiet words on the kraft wall: the date, its help lines, the footer), `--paper` and `--bg` (surfaces), `--muted` and `--faint` (quiet text), `--line` (dividers), `--accent` and `--accent-ink` (the book's orange), `--tag` (needs attention: a filled chip, a due line), `--done` and `--done-ink` (saved, ticked), `--bar` and `--bar-text` (the dark top bar and tab bar).
- **Operational colours** (whole-app council, 9 November 2026): retain orange for actions, selected notebook controls and attention, green for completion or explicit assignment, and neutral charcoal/grey for ordinary information. Use `--bad-ink` and `--bad-tint` for failed reads/writes, invalid entries, cancellation and destructive actions. On charcoal use `--bad-on-dark` and `--done-on-dark` so status text stays readable. Colour always accompanies existing words, ticks, pressed states or outlines; an assignment in a draft is not a confirmed save. Preserve the named charcoal Urgent flag. Claimed Prep work keeps full text contrast, with the claimant's neutral badge and name. Food drawings, allergen certainty, wastage reasons, chart scales, seasonal art and approved card surfaces have separate meanings. See [the inventory and council](colour-coding.md).
- **Surface colour** (owner-approved hybrid, 9 November 2026): coloured covers, headers and spines identify purpose while writing areas stay quiet. Use `--surface-work` and `--surface-work-edge` for work, including Orders; `--surface-people` and `--surface-people-edge` for people; `--surface-paper` for interiors; and `--surface-reference` for quiet reference and forms. Current live boards and bands retain charcoal. Home retains its approved purpose surfaces. These surface identities do not imply due, done or Saved. Preserve category, chart, art and print palettes. [Chosen contract, council and exceptions](surface-colours.md).
- **Attention and outcome fills** remain local: the top bar chip until today's team is saved, an attention banner, a filled tag or an acknowledged result. Amber means attention or pending, green confirms the stated success and red marks errors or existing adverse/destructive actions. Retain explicit assignment/inclusion labels on existing green controls. Groups use space and headings first; the approved purpose surfaces are a bounded exception, not a reason to box every group.
- **Notebook colour** (Chef to-do and Today's team, updated 9 November 2026): keep the approved work or people cover and spine, warm writing paper, orange active choices, green ticks and explicitly completed or assigned states, and quiet neutral dividers. Use `--small-ink` for small orange text and selected fills with `--bar-text`; `--done-ink` for completion; `--faint` for readable control outlines. Local attention and scheduled-away cues retain their words. Colour reinforces checkmarks, pressed states and strikethrough. Preserve the approved spacing, target sizes and acknowledgement timing.
- **Type:** `--display` (Barlow Condensed) for headings and labels, `--body` (Barlow) for the rest, Caveat for the handwritten done count (`.nb-prog`).
- **Corners:** `--r-act` (8 px) on buttons that do something (Save, Add), `--r-pick` (round) on chips and filters that choose.
- **Tap targets** are at least 44 px; a quiet secondary link may be smaller when a 44 px way to the same place is on screen. A control drawn smaller on purpose, in a bar where every pixel counts, keeps its look and takes a 44 px tap through an `::after` that reaches past it, so the bar grows no taller: the function band's minus, its "+N more" and Service view, and the cut-off bar's supplier name (consistency sweep, 19 Nov 2026). Test it with a tap 20 px from the middle (`tests/service-targets.test.js`), not the box's size.
- **Icons** are inline SVG with `currentColor` and `aria-hidden="true"`, from `ICON` in `js/app.js`; an icon drawn more than once with a mask gets its own mask id each time (`tmIcon()`).

## A page

- **The functions strip** (19 Nov 2026, [brief](band-options/brief.md)): one 44 px line in the band's charcoal.
  - It starts with `.fn-strip-l` (the word Functions), then the lead in words, tags first so a narrow screen cuts the name and never them, then "+N" apart, then a chevron.
  - `stripItems()` puts a cancelled, then a rescheduled, then a changed function, then a trip waiting for this Functions person, first. Every other function and the order and tomorrow lines are counted in "+N".
  - `stripInner()` draws it, with `.fn-strip-stale` and the band's own words from `staleWords()` when the copy is old or a read failed.
  - It pins to the top once the band scrolls away, on every page.
  - On Prep the band itself starts as the strip (`prepStrip()`, `.fn-band.is-strip`, `.fn-strip-in`). A tap opens the service view with two or more functions on, otherwise the sheet.
- **Prep keeps your place** (19 Nov 2026):
  - `prepPlaceSave()` keeps the line at the top of the screen and its offset, in memory, for one person, day, section and board.
  - `prepPlaceBack()` puts it back once, after Prep has drawn, and never while a finger is down.
  - Tapping Prep while on Prep goes to the top. A reload or the 04:00 refresh starts at the top.
- **Main-tab touch navigation** uses `pageSwipeFrom()` and the existing permitted `navList()` in `js/app.js`. A clear horizontal swipe moves one main tab without wrapping. `.page-swipe-surface` follows the finger slightly inside `.page-swiping`, then settles in 180 ms. Header, tabs and fixed controls stay anchored. Reduced motion removes movement. Fields, sheets, selected text, zoom and scrollable rows retain their own gestures. [Contract](navigation.md#main-tab-swipes-15-november-2026).

- **The card:** `section.p-card` with `h2.p-h` (id `rtitle`, so the page's Back and focus land on it), a `.hint` line for what the page is for and when it was read, then the work. Buttons sit in a `.btn-row`: `.btn` for most, `.btn.dark` for the one main action. A quiet action in a sentence is a `.p-link` (Refresh, Try again).
- **A failed read** never shows "nothing", "nobody" or "0": a `.p-err` line with role status says it could not load, a Try again, and the book tries once more after 5 minutes by itself (`retrySoon()`, `waiting()`). Try again runs the read the book would run after its wait (`readRetry(k)`), or the page's own reload given as `attr`, so a second tap never starts a second read. No signal is said only when the signal is the cause (consistency sweep, step 2, 20 Nov 2026). No signal on its own is not a failure: it is a neutral note that says what still works (Orders' `.nb-empty` note, Delivery received's `.dl-offline`, the supplier order's `.su-offline`), never the red failure box. The freshness line on the Functions pages sits right under the heading on every page. An answer from the phone's own copy with no signal is not "none" (`cacheOnly()`).

## Choosing

- **Two to about six fixed choices:** chips in a row (`.chip` with `aria-pressed`), one tap. A count on a chip goes in a `small` inside it (Today's team's shift chips).
- **Configured shift tabs with a day review** (Today's team, retained by C Person worksheet chosen 9 Nov 2026): `.tm-viewbar` keeps Whole day visible beside `.tm-shift-scroll`, a row of configured shifts that scrolls within the page and leaves the page itself fitting the screen. Whole day reviews the draft; it is never a shift ID. Choose a real shift, then use each person's own section picker. Name the shift and person in controls. Changing or clearing a section affects that shift only and keeps membership and breaks. Not on the named shift is an explicit removal choice and removes only that shift's breaks. All views keep the same draft and Save.
- **More, or a list from the kitchen's settings:** a `<select>` that the shared controls dress as a picker (`LK_UX.enhance`, `.ux-picker`): a bottom sheet on a phone, a centred box from 768 px.
- **A time typed in a form** (function times, head chef 18 Nov 2026, [brief](fn-time-picker-options/brief.md)): a typed box with a number keypad (`.fe-time` in `.fe-tcell`), read by one reader that never guesses: 1830, 0630, 18, 6:30pm are taken, always 24-hour; 1 to 11 with no am or pm asks **Morning or evening?** (`.fe-tq`, two `.chip` in `.fe-tamb`, the box edged in `--tag`); a time it cannot read is a red `.fe-err` line. While typing, `.fe-tsay` says the time back ("18:30 evening") where there is no list. On a laptop (from 768 px with a mouse) a box that sets the main time drops **a list of times** under it (`.fe-tl`, a listbox of `.fe-tl-o` rows every 15 minutes, 44 px, the box's time marked with an orange edge, the arrow-key row in ink), narrowed as typed; the box is a combobox with `aria-activedescendant`. A press on the list keeps the focus in the box. Never inside a scrolling table, never on a phone (the keyboard covers it). Nothing saves, confirms or prints while a time is unclear.
- **A day: the day control** (head chef, 20 Nov 2026, the consistency sweep, step 3): `‹ Today date ›` in `.nb-nav`, drawn by `dayNavHTML()` in `js/prep.js`: a back step chip, the Today chip (pressed on today), the date box (`.p-dateinput`) and a forward step chip. Prep and Orders (chefs) use it; on Orders it sits in `.o-dayctl` with Delivery received after it on today (under it on a phone, where the row is full: one row of 52 px more than the date box alone). The `‹` and `›` are **step chips** (`.chip.nav-step`, 48 by 44 px, large type) wherever a day or a week is stepped: Cleaning's week (`.cln-week-nav`, its words kept) and Today's team's week arrows too. Today's team keeps its own date picker (`.tm-date-controls`, below): each of its days says Draft, Saved or Not set from one bounded week read, which a date box cannot show.
- **More than about 12:** the picker gets a type-to-find box with the likely few first (`AdaptivePicker` in `js/controls.js`).
- **Fill in what the book knows** and keep it easy to change: the usual section, the unit, last week's team (Use it), a long shift's split. A suggestion fills every field it knows. Nothing is saved by itself.
- **Filled for you** (head chef, 13 Nov 2026, `docs/smart-book-options/brief.md`): when the book fills a box from what it knows (an item typed in full on Wastage or Transfers, the usual order amount), the box carries a word tag inside it (`.lk-fb`, `.lk-ftag`: LAST TIME or USUAL, a neutral word in a box, never green or orange), a label carries the same tag, and one line right under the boxes (`.lk-fillnote`) says what was filled and where from, with a 44 px button to empty it. Draw with `fbHTML()` and `ftagHTML()`; a fill started by leaving a box waits for the finger to come up (`fillAfterTap()`) and patches in place, so the tap that left the box still lands.

## Saving and after the tap
