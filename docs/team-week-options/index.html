Planning sections ahead

Give next week a place in the book.

Three ways for head and sous chefs to choose a day, assign sections and save its plan. Each uses the same fictional team. Nothing here changes the real kitchen.

A. Extend the notebook

A compact seven-day strip above the familiar roster. Tap Friday, choose Grill, then tap the people on it. The saved plan sits on the other notebook page.

Best for: staying close to Today’s team.
Cost: an extra row of dates.

Try A at full size

B. See the whole week

A week board shows section cover before you edit. Seven columns on a laptop become clear day summaries on a phone. Open a day to assign people.

Best for: spotting gaps across days.
Cost: an extra step and a longer phone view.

Try B at full size

C. Plan in the chef panel

Team → Section planning. One date picker with previous and next day. A focused editor keeps the week strip and live break board elsewhere.

Best for: a quiet place to plan.
Cost: more navigation from Today’s team.

Try C at full size

In every option: drafts stay with their date as you move around, nothing saves automatically, and Save names the full date. Staff’s service screens continue to use today.

These are visual and interaction previews. They have no Firebase connection or production save. Dates and sample allocations are fixed for comparison. Font rendering uses the browser fallback if the book’s fonts are unavailable.
