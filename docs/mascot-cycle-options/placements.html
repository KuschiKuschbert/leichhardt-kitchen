Where the mascot could stand

Preview, 9 November 2026. The real Home page with synthetic staff (Ann, Lunch on Larder) and the test stand-in for Firebase, at phone 390 px, tablet 768 px and laptop 1280 px. The mascot shows its resting Welcome pose; on the first Home view of the day it would play today's action once in the same spot. No Home card moves in any of them. The live book is unchanged.

1. Heading corner

Far right of the "Welcome" row, under the top bar. The spot the council reviewed. On a laptop it sits a long way from the name.

2. Beside the name

Just after "Welcome, Ann", feet on the date line, so it reads as part of the greeting. A long name leaves no room and the mascot is left out that day.

3. End of Home

Standing on a ledge after all of Home's work, right aligned, before the footer line. It never sits near the board, but on a phone you only see it when you scroll to the bottom. It adds about 90 to 140 px to the end of the page.

What the celebration shows

  • Christmas brings snow. On the first Home view of the day about 40 small flakes fall for about 4 seconds, mostly down the left and right edges, then a few slow flakes stay. That first view is also when the mascot plays today's action, so the two arrive together.
  • The heading corner sits in the right-hand snow lane: 3 or 4 flakes pass it during the arrival at every width. Beside the name stays almost clear (0 on phone and laptop, 1 on tablet). The flakes fall behind the page's words and the mascot.
  • On a named Home the celebration keeps its hanging and standing ornaments out of the heading area (the welcome rule), so no ornament touches any spot.
  • Christmas adds its chalkboard card under the date. Placements 1 and 2 stay above it; placement 3 moves down with the page.
  • Snow arriving is a single frame, part way through the fall. End of Home has no arrival image: you would be at the top of the page while the snow falls.
