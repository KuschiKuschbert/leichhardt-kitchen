What I found

The current pages, on a phone

Supplier order

  • Counting is hidden.Stock on hand sits inside each item's Details. You open an item to count it, close it, then type the order.
  • Suggestions are far from the item.They live in a closed panel above the list, not on the row they are for.
  • 20 items a page.A supplier with 60 items is three pages, and Review order is only at the bottom.
  • Staff requests are a small tag.The row says "Staff request to check" but not who asked, for what or how much.
  • Rows are tall.Name, pack, Details, Par, To confirm, Last order, a box and a unit hint: about five items per phone screen.
  • No view of the day.Nothing shows which suppliers have a draft, are ordered or are due in.

Delivery received

  • Two boxes look required.Chiller and freezer sit side by side. The hint that one is enough is easy to miss.
  • Two keypads.The chiller box opens the number pad. The freezer box opens the full keyboard so the minus sign can be reached.
  • The supplier is typed every time.One supplier typed three ways becomes three suppliers in any report.
  • The book knows who is due, but does not say.The order cut-offs already hold each supplier's delivery days.
  • Save is out of reach.It sits under the notes box, behind the bottom tabs until you scroll.
  • Admin sees one date at a time.No week view, no supplier filter, no CSV.

Two designs

Pick a direction

A · The Order Sheet

Keep both pages and the way they work now. Tidy each supplier row so counting and ordering happen in one place, and turn Delivery received into a one-reading form that knows who is due.

  • Saved data and rules: no change.
  • Reads: none new when a page opens. Admin range is one read on tap.
  • Ships as: three small pull requests, deliveries first.
  • Undo: revert any one of them.

B · The Supplier Docket

Each supplier gets a docket that moves from draft to ordered to arriving to received, like the landing page dockets. Ordering becomes Count, Order, Record. Receiving checks the order line by line.

  • Saved data and rules: new fields for the order check and your temperature limits. Backups and PIN moves updated too.
  • Reads: one read of the expected order when a chef receives it.
  • Ships as: five to six pull requests, rules first.
  • Undo: screens revert; saved checks and their rules stay.

Recommendation

Build A now. Keep B's order check for later.

  1. It fixes what slows people today. One temperature box, one tap for the supplier, count and order on one row.

  2. Nothing saved changes. Receipts, orders and the rules stay exactly as they are, so each part ships on its own and a revert is one pull request.

  3. The actions stay distinct. Record order placed, Order done, Apply and Save delivery keep separate buttons and outcomes, as the audit asked.

  4. Nothing in A is thrown away. The expected-supplier chips, the On hand column and the Review bar are the parts B would build on.

  5. B needs your numbers first. Its value comes from temperature limits and order checks. Those are easier to set after a few weeks of clean records with matching supplier names.

Your calls

What I need from you

  • A or B.Or A now and B's order check later.
  • A starting choice for What came in?Start on Chilled every time, or remember each supplier's last choice on that phone. Nothing is guessed when there is no last choice.
  • For B only: the limits.The Food Standards Code (Standard 3.2.2, clause 5) says chilled food that needs temperature control is received at 5 °C or colder, and frozen food arrives frozen. The book sets no number until you confirm yours.

Stays the same in both

What does not change

  • A saved delivery never changes. A correction is a new receipt with a note.
  • One reading or both, zero and negative readings save exactly as typed.
  • Record order placed sends nothing to the supplier. Order done, Apply and Save delivery stay separate.
  • The catalogue, pars and order history stay with the head chef, sous chefs and admin. Staff never see order lines.
  • Every role keeps the access it has now. No new outside service, font or polling.
