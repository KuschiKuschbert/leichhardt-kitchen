Two ways to upgrade each work page

Prep, Orders, Wastage, Transfers and the chef hat menu. Each one shows today's phone screen next to Option A and Option B, with what goes, what is new and what it costs. My recommendation is at the end. Nothing is built yet. You choose, then I build it in small steps you can undo.

A
Clean notebook

Same pages, same look. The list comes first and one Add button sits at thumb height above the tab bar. The repeats go. Lowest risk, nothing new to learn.

B
Service rail

Each page rebuilt around its job during service: your section first, suppliers and cut-offs, one-line logging, a ledger per venue. More new features, more change for the team, and some need rules work.

Names and amounts are the book's test data. Phone numbers were measured on a 390 by 844 px screen with a sous chef signed in.

In both options

Six shared fixes

These go in whichever option you pick. They remove most of the repeats on their own. Client only: no rules change and no new reads.

S1
Say who once

Drop the Writing as row. The chef hat shows your initial, and Not you? moves to the top of its menu. Shared screens keep the name next to the hat.

S2
One section control

One row of the kitchen's sections as chips, today's section already picked, and Other… to type a new one. It replaces the box, Choose Section and the tag.

S3
One amount control

One box that reads "2 tubs" or "2", with unit chips under it, on all four pages. The book already reads typed amounts.

S4
One line layout

Tick on the left, words and amount, a small second line (section, who, time), ··· on the right. The same on all four lists.

S5
Fix a line

New: Change amount and Move to section in the ··· menu, with Undo. The rules already let people with Add change both. An emulator check comes first.

S6
One meaning for usual chips

On Prep and Orders a usual chip adds the line in one tap, with Undo. On Wastage and Transfers it fills the form and waits for the amount, because amounts vary.

Prep list

The notebook works, but it asks a lot of a phone. A sous chef's first line starts 519 px down, under 14 controls, and the add box is 1,456 px down. There are four ways to add a line and four places that count what is done.

A

Clean notebook

The notebook you know, with one way to add and one place for done.

  • The list starts under one slim header: Today, 1 of 13 done and a thin progress line.
  • Section tabs carry their own counts (Grill 0/2).
  • Start here is marked on the list itself: the first three jobs get 1, 2 and 3. The separate strip goes.
  • Urgent is one control. The ! turns orange and the URGENT badge goes.
  • Add a line sits above the tab bar. Its sheet finds usual lines and recipe cards as you type, then section, amount and Add. It replaces Write a line, the foot form, Add from the recipes and Quick add.
  • Tap 1 of 13 done for Done today, Undo and Top tickers.
  • Laptop: the two-page notebook stays. The right page becomes the Add panel, always open, with Done today under it.

New Lines written after the night batch get their own heading, Tonight, for tomorrow. Change amount and Move section in ··· (S5).

Cost Client only. No rules change, no new reads.

Try it in the phone: tap Add a line, or 1 of 13 done.

B

My section first

Opens on the job in front of you.

  • Opens on the section you picked after your PIN. Its jobs come first. Other sections fold to one line each with their progress. Chefs switch to All.
  • Next up: the most important open job on a docket, with a big Tick it and I'm on it.
  • Add is one line above the tab bar, already set to your section, with that section's usual lines as one-tap chips.
  • A tablet on the wall shows one column per section, like dockets on the rail.

New I'm on it puts a name on a job, so two cooks do not do the same one. Next up. A wall board view for the tablet.

Cost I'm on it needs a new field and a rules change, published before the app. Works best once everyone picks a section every day.

Try it in the phone: tap a section under Other sections.

A

Clean list

One list, oldest worries visible, add from the thumb.

  • One list. Today's requests, then Waiting from earlier days, which replaces the Everything to order tab for chefs. One tick still marks every line for that item.
  • Ordered today folds to one line at the foot.
  • Add to the order opens the same sheet as Prep: type to find, Usually ordered on Tuesdays chips that add in one tap, amount and unit.
  • Chefs: other days sit behind one date chip in the header. Today's supplier cut-offs are one strip.

B

By supplier

Requests sorted by who delivers them and when the order closes.

  • Chefs see requests under each supplier's cut-off: PFD by 14:00, 2 h 10 min left.
  • Open PFD order starts that supplier's draft with these lines. Nothing is recorded until you Record it there.
  • Delivery arrived opens Delivery received for that supplier, so it is no longer only in the chef hat menu.
  • Lines the book cannot match go under No supplier yet, with Pick supplier, remembered next time.
  • Staff mark how urgent it is, Low or Out, amount optional. Out goes to the top for chefs. Staff never see suppliers.

New Supplier groups with a countdown, Low and Out, the Delivery arrived link. Supplier orders could be ticked off here only (where chefs tick is your call).

Cost The biggest change. Low and Out need a new field and rules. Grouping reads each supplier's catalogue on the order list for chefs, about one read per supplier a day, so the read budget changes.

Wastage

Logging is careful but long. On a phone there are 16 controls before the first entry, so today's entries start under the tab bar. Section is a box plus a Choose button, and the reason is a dropdown on phones but a different picker on laptops.

A

Log sheet

See the day first, log from the thumb.

  • Today first: the total, the entries and a bar that splits the kilos by reason.
  • Log waste above the tab bar opens the sheet: Logged often, what, amount and unit, the five reasons as big chips with their hint, section already set.
  • The button names what it saves: Log 2 kg of Chips.
  • The rule line (the actual amount, never a dollar value) moves into the sheet, under the amount.
  • Chefs: the End-of-day review becomes one button at the foot that opens its own sheet.
  • The same checks as today: the big-amount question, Undo for chefs, and "Wrong? Ask the head chef" for staff.

New The reason bar, worked out from today's lines.

Cost Client only. No new reads.

Try it in the phone: tap Log waste.

B

One-line log

Say it like you would on the line.

  • One sentence at the top: Wasted Chips, 2 kg, Excess production, from Fryer. Each word is a chip. Tap one to change it. Logged often fills the whole sentence.
  • Today's entries sit right under it, on the first screen.
  • After a Cooking fault or Expired stock entry: Make it again? Add to prep, one tap with Undo.
  • Chefs: a Tomorrow card from the nightly summary. "Chips: excess 7 times in 14 days. Prep one tub less?" Add to the review fills the review box, and you still save it.

New Make it again, a link from Wastage to Prep. The Tomorrow card. Sentence entry.

Cost Client only. Uses the nightly summary the book already reads once a day. The chips in a sentence are smaller targets than A's.

Try it in the phone: tap Excess production to see the reasons.

Transfers

Every transfer starts with a long form. On a phone Log it is 874 px down, below the first screen, and Still outstanding and today's list come after it. A pick-up of three items means three rounds of fill in and Log it. The return tick sits on the right, while every other list ticks on the left.

A

Pick-up sheet

What is still out comes first. A whole pick-up in one go.

  • What is still out comes first: earlier days with how long they have been out, then today. The tick is on the left, like every other list.
  • Today's transfers sit under it.
  • Log a transfer opens the sheet: They took from us or We took from them, venue chips, person chips, then a basket. Add every item in the pick-up, each with its amount and unit.
  • Log 2 items to Riviera saves them together. The note stays optional, once per pick-up.

New Several items in one save. Each item is still its own line, so the rules, the admin report, returns and the CSV stay the same.

Cost Client only. A three-item pick-up goes from three forms to one.

Try it in the phone: tap Log a transfer.

B

Venue ledger

Who has what, venue by venue.

  • Tabs by venue, each with its balance: Riviera 3 out, Grand Hotel 1 in.
  • Each venue shows what they have of ours and what we have of theirs, oldest first, with an age tag after 7 days.
  • One line to log, with the venue already set: Riviera took what, how much, and who came.
  • Chefs get a count on the chef hat for anything out 7 days or more.

New A balance per venue, age tags, the overdue count.

Cost Client only. Uses the open-loans listener and today's lines the page already has. Venues come from the nightly summary. Risk: logging from the wrong venue tab.

Try it in the phone: tap Grand Hotel.
