# Quieter Today's team

9 November 2026. Proposal for council review. No tidy-up design chosen.

The head chef first requested hiding On break now when nobody is on a break, then added: "aaand can we tidy up the today team admin/head chef/souschef page it looks quite noisy". The empty-board change is independently authorised and being implemented. It hides only a current, successfully checked, valid saved plan with no active meal break or split. Loading, failed, stale, unset or malformed status remains visible. Clock paints can bring the board back without a read or losing drafts.

The broader problem is duplicated information and competing controls inside the management planner. The selected date appears above the notebook and in its heading, followed by a clock and timezone. Week navigation, a live break board, shift tabs, a draft explanation, section chips, assignment instructions, a search box and large people rows all precede another full list of the same people under open Breaks and fine adjustments. Save follows that second list and the batch-break fold. On desktop a second saved-plan summary repeats the plan. Inside the chef panel the planner also sits below the outer room/page headings and navigation.

The existing useful core must remain: head, sous and verified-admin authority; Monday to Sunday and previous/next week plus Today; separate per-date drafts; section-first allocation or an equally clear alternative; configured shifts; Whole day review; meal breaks and splits; selected-weekday copy; Spread them; one explicit dated Save, failures and version conflicts; correct saved break status; read-only past dates; no future-plan leakage into today's staff screens. Use the existing book palette, fonts, responsive frame and controls. No payroll, new global tab, listener, extra database read or new backend is proposed.

Proposal: reduce duplicate headings and helper text, show people once in the main planning flow, and put detailed break/shift tools behind deliberate disclosure. Compare three different ways to do that: a restrained version of the existing section-first notebook, a board grouped by section, and a single worksheet by person. These are alternatives, not a recommendation. All need the same underlying capabilities and clear dated Save.

Cost: existing js/prep.js shared planner and css/app.css, focused browser checks and current team/role/read-cost contracts. The shared component already serves direct Today's team and the chef-panel Plan page, including canonical head and sous roles; verified admin reaches it through existing links. Do not create three editors or a second source of truth. Main risks: hiding critical saved/unsaved status, adding taps for breaks, moving familiar assignment controls, losing the selected date, collapsing something a person deliberately opened, and disturbing typed drafts during clock repaint.

Baseline screenshots in this folder show the real current app with eight fictional people and illustrative configured shifts, through tests/lib.js and stand-in Firebase at 390 and 1280 px, direct team and embedded chef routes. Actual Barlow and Caveat fonts are supplied from verified downloaded font responses. Nobody's real private records are read or seeded. See baseline-measurements.json for measured initial visibility and errors; screenshots are baseline-team-390.png, baseline-team-1280.png, baseline-chef-390.png and baseline-chef-1280.png.
