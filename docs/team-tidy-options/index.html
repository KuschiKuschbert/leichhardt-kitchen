The team, with room to breathe.

Three ways to tidy the management planner. Every option keeps the week, shifts, sections, breaks, Whole day and one dated Save. Each person appears once in the main flow.

A. Quiet notebook

Keep section-first assignment, with each person shown once. Their row holds the section and a short break summary. Open the arrow for attendance, breaks and splits.

Tradeoff: Closest to the current flow. Break edits gain one deliberate opening tap.

B. Section board

See the people under Grill, Pans and Larder. Assign from each section’s inline chooser. Section columns on a laptop become groups on a phone.

Tradeoff: Strong for checking cover. Adding or moving someone starts with that section’s Assign.

Try B at full size

C. Person worksheet

One row per person with a section picker already visible. Breaks stay as short summaries; the arrow opens the details.

Tradeoff: Easy for checking individuals. Repeatedly assigning several people takes more picker taps.

All options keep separate drafts as you change dates. Nothing saves automatically. The compact charcoal board shows saved current breaks only, and stays out of the way when nobody is away.
