What happens next

  1. The head chef picks A, B or C, or a mix (for example A, with C's two-column chef hat menu).
  2. The build goes in small pull requests, each with click tests at 390, 768 and 1280 px and every role walked through. No rules change and no new reads for A or C.
  3. Nothing moves out of the admin panel unless B is picked. Every setting keeps the save it has now.
