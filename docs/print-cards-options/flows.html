Print recipe cards: three ways to choose

Head chef, sous chefs and admin only (owner, 19 Nov 2026). Every design opens the phone's or computer's own print dialog last, prints base amounts, and repeats the card number, name, page x of y, allergens and the print time on every page. Synthetic preview: the real book's top bar and tab bar are drawn as plain bars.

A. Print from the card

Changes the least. A Print chip on the recipe card's trail line, beside Keep screen on, for chefs only. One small sheet: which paper, and this card alone or with the recipes it uses. No way to print many cards at once. Layout A: the card in the screen's order.

B. A print list in Manage Recipes

Rethinks the flow. Print cards sits at the top of Manage Recipes (head chef, sous chefs, admin already). It turns the recipe list into a tick list: tick cards one by one, or a quick pick (a menu section, Batch recipes, Everything). A bar at the bottom keeps the count, the paper and Print. Layout B: a paper card, drawing and plate key in a side column, ingredients then method in the wide one.

C. Card packs in the chef panel

Removes the most friction for the usual jobs. A Print cards tile in the Head chef / Sous chef panel opens one sheet of ready packs: the specials, each menu section, batch recipes, everything, or Pick cards. The paper is remembered on this device. Layout C: a station card, big ingredients and allergens to read from a metre away, the method small underneath.
