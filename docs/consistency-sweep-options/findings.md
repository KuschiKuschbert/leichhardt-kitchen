# Consistency findings register (19 November 2026)

The register behind [the brief](brief.md). Nine auditors (eight page families and the shared CSS system) compared every page with `docs/design-patterns.md`, `docs/surface-colours.md`, `docs/colour-coding.md`, `docs/action-feedback.md` and the sibling pages, from source and from screenshots of the app as built (main `83f8ddd`, synthetic fixture, 390 and 1280 px, head chef and staff). An independent verifier per family then tried to refute each finding and checked `docs/head-chef-decisions.md` and the feature briefs for deliberate choices. Role-based analysis, not research with real staff.

Severity: **service** slows or confuses a task in service or risks a wrong action; **confusing** a new starter could misread it; **cosmetic** looks different with no effect on the task. Fix: **tiny** a line or two, **small** one page and under a day, **rework** the page needs redrawing (none found). Choice: fixing it needs the head chef to choose a look or behaviour.

Missed by the auditor, found by the verifier:

- Now and next's chevron is practically invisible. Board rows with no time ('Minutes to sign', a ping) draw .hm-chev in hard-coded rgba(251, 248, 241, 0.7) (css/app.css:5004). That colour was made for the old charcoal board. The amber reskin (css 5075-5079) remaps the --bar tokens but not this literal, so the chevron is cream on #FFF1E6, about 1:1. A crop of head-1280-home.png at the Minutes to sign row (around x 1180, y 845) shows only a faint ghost. phone-screens.md and js/prep.js:12227 promise 'rows with no time show a chevron ... it opens something'. Drift, confusing, a one-line fix (currentColor or a token).
- SHEL-3 overstates the case. installCard() is drawn after the .hm board (js/app.js:838-840), not above it, so at most two cards can come before Your day. The late-minutes card also takes the place of the board's own 'Minutes to sign' row by design (js/prep.js:12221, shown only when minNudgeHTML() is empty). So when minutes go late, the same item jumps from an amber board row to a differently framed card above Your day (css 1361) with a non-.btn ink button (css 1359). That jump is the concrete inconsistency.
- SHEL-1's argument about shape is contradicted by the book itself. The laptop page tabs (.pnav a, css 724, radius 999px) are round pills that navigate, so 'round means choosing' is not how the book works in practice. Only the 36 px size breaks a stated contract (design-patterns.md:20).
- The hat menu's real breakpoint differs from its comments and from the top bar's. css 5463-5470 keep it a bottom sheet up to 767 px and centre it from 768 px, but the comments say 'a panel under the hat from 720 px' (css 1837, js/app.js:610). The top bar switches to laptop tabs at 720 px. Between 720 and 767 px a tablet therefore has laptop tabs with a phone-style hat sheet. This was judged from source only, as there are no 768 px captures.

Limits: No 768 px screenshots, so the tablet top bar (720 to 1099 px media rules) was judged from source only. Sheets and menus were not captured: the chef hat menu, the Your day sheet, Find and the Settings rows when open were judged from source. Not captured at all: signed-out Home, Functions-only Home, a shared-device top bar with a name, staff Home at 1280 px, no-signal and failed-read states, the band folded, the Next function line (a function was on today), the Function this week card, seasonal celebrations (off in the fixture) and the April Fools Home. Tap sizes for .cut-n come from the CSS and screenshot proportions, not from measuring in a browser. Contrast was judged by eye, not measured. Admin-side pages that Settings links to belong to another family.
