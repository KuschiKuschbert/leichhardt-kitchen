# The kitchen mascot: on Home, under a finished prep list, and on a finished section's screen

Built 9 November 2026 (owner). The Smashed Burger star robot with the navy bandana greets each named person on Home. The artwork study, the council rounds, the placement mock and their evidence are in pull request #654 (`docs/kitchen-mascot/`, `docs/mascot-placement-options/`, `docs/mascot-cycle-options/` on branch `feat/kitchen-mascot-animations`).

## What it does

- **A shuffled action each day, per person.** Each signed-in person has their own shuffled set of all 11 actions: Idle, Welcome, Look around, Recipe reading, Ready to cook, Smash, Flip, Build, Serve, Walk and At the smoker. No action repeats for that person until all 11 have played, then the set reshuffles. A new set never starts with the action shown last, so no action shows two days running.
- **Once, then still.** On the first Home view of the kitchen day where it can play, today's action plays once (2.5 to 3.9 seconds), then the mascot rests on the Welcome pose. Returning to Home the same day opens at rest.
- **Tap to replay.** A tap replays today's action. It never draws a new one or changes the set. A second tap while it plays does nothing. With reduced motion a tap shows today's pose as a still for 2 seconds, then the Welcome pose.
- **Where.** The right end of the "Welcome, [name]" row, standing on the date line, under the top bar and above the first card. 64 x 85 px, or 48 x 64 px when that is all that fits. The daily action is Home only: never on Prep, Orders, Recipes or any work page. The other places he stands are under a finished prep list and, on a wide screen, on a finished section's own tab (both below).
- **When the words fill the corner** (owner, 10 November 2026). The varied welcome (#656, #661) often reaches the corner on a phone. The owner wanted the mascot to stay visible, and chose B from three mocked spots (A keep the corner, B on the Your day card, C in the top bar):
  1. The corner, 64 or 48 px, as before.
  2. Otherwise on the Your day card's header line, left of its clock, peeking over the card top. Nothing on Home moves. 48 x 64 px, or 44 x 59 px, which stays under the date line when a wide date ("Wednesday 30 September", or "08:30") reaches across. Clear of the date by 6 px, the card title, the card's pin and the top bar.
  3. Otherwise, where Home has no Your day card (Functions-only), the heading and date keep 76 px of room (`.mascot-room`) and their words wrap before the corner. The heading can grow by a line.
  4. Left out only when none of these clears every word, a seasonal ornament and the top bar. The full name and date always show.
- **It follows the words.** A new greeting (`lk-greet`), a seasonal change, a rename or a resize asks it to check its place again. A greeting or seasonal change only moves a mascot already on Home (`mascotPlace()` in `js/app.js`); it never mounts a new one, so missing art is not fetched again until the next Home view (it was fetched 50 times in a few seconds before this check). The room is measured without itself first, so a resize never loops.

## Under a finished prep list

Built 10 November 2026 (owner: "yes i trust your recommendations", after the service council, [the mascot cheer study](mascot-cheer-options/brief.md), first in pull request #670: round 1 Build smaller, 5 of 5; round 2, five of six rank this design first).

- **What shows.** When every line of today's own prep list is ticked and the server has every tick, the mascot stands beside the words where the page already says the list is ticked off: "Every line ticked. 6 of 6." in a green speech shape, above the page's own "See Done today under Handy." The finish is said once. In the page, never over it, and not a control.
- **A state, not an event.** It is worked out from the lines the phone already holds (`finOk()` in `js/prep.js`). Nothing shows while a line is left, or while any change to the day's own lines is waiting to be sent or refused, a removal included. Undo, a new line, another phone's tick and a reload all correct it by themselves, with no flag to keep in step.
- **One play.** On the phone whose tick finished the list he plays Serve once, after the ticked lines have left (3.5 seconds), at most once a kitchen day on that device. Then he rests on the Welcome pose. Every other phone, and a reload, show him as a still. The phone remembers its own finishing tick only in the tab's memory, for that person, for two minutes, and only while that line still shows as ticked by them (`finStill()`): an Undo, a refusal, a handover or a tick that never reached the server ends it.
- **Where it does not show.** A list of one line (an ordinary tick), the wall board, another day, a function's platter tab. Those keep their plain words. A section's own tab keeps its plain words under 900 px; from 900 px wide a finished section has its own finished words there (below).
- **Size.** 48 x 64 px where Handy sits under the list (under 900 px), so the page's words stay on one line beside him, with his room kept before the art loads and when it is missing, so nothing under him moves. From 900 px wide the card says it large with him under his words, up to his drawn size (below); where the large words do not fit, with no signal, or with an older saved stylesheet, it is the small card at 64 x 85 px.

## A finished section, on the slip

Built 10 November 2026 (owner: "yeah go ahead", the same evening step 1 went live). This is step 2 of the council's recommendation in [the mascot cheer study](mascot-cheer-options/brief.md). The council split three to three on it, and the recommendation was to let step 1 run about four weeks first; the owner chose to go ahead. Words only: the mascot is not on the slip.

- **What shows.** The slip that answers a tick ("Ticked Laksa", Undo, 9 seconds) gains one line of words under it when that tick finished a section: "Sauce is done. Grill has 1 left." It names the section and what is next, never a person, the clock or speed. A name that ends in one s, or joins two things, reads as more than one: "Pans are done. Fryers have 2 left."
- **Only after the server has the tick.** The words come when the tick's own write is answered (`secSay()` in `js/prep.js`), never from the listener alone and never from a cached answer. A tick waiting to be sent shows the plain slip; a refused tick shows the book's red slip only. On the shared tablet the slip is only made once the server has answered, so the words come with it.
- **Only that tick's own slip, never late.** The words live on the tick's own Undo. An answer that comes when the slip has under 3 seconds left, has gone, has been replaced by a newer slip, or is not on screen (the cook opened a recipe or another page) says nothing, then or later. The slip's 9 seconds are not extended.
- **True on every screen, or not said.** The book counts a section three ways: its heading on All, its own tab (which also holds lines listed under it as an also section) and Home. So a section is done only when every line of it anywhere is ticked (`secLines()`), with two lines or more, and nothing of the day's own list waiting to be sent. On All the section is the ticked line's own; on a section's own tab, where Home sends each cook, it is that tab's.
- **What is next.** The first section in the All list's own order that has a line left and that All, its own tab and Home count the same (`secNext()`): none of its open lines is only listed under it, and none was written tonight for tomorrow. A section they count differently is passed over. When lines are left but no section can be named for them, nothing is said, so the words never read as "nothing is left". "Sauce is done." alone shows only on a section's own tab when its last tick is also the list's last.
- **Names it does not say.** A line with no section; a section called All, Done, Prep, List, Kitchen, Service, Function or Functions, which would read as the whole list, the kitchen or the Functions tab; a time of day (Today, Tonight, Tomorrow, Morning, Night, Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner); a section that is the name or first name of a person on the staff list.
- **Decided once.** At the server's answer, for the view the cook is on. After that it is only checked on every draw (`secWords()`), and taken away for good when it stops being true (a line added to the section or unticked on another phone), or when the cook moves to another tab or page, Home and a recipe card included (`leftPrep()`). The count follows the list while it shows.
- **Once a kitchen day for each section on a device,** kept once the words have stood 2 seconds. A wrong tick undone straight away, or words replaced by the next slip before anyone could read them, do not spend it. The words are read out at the same moment, after the slip's own.
- **One line or nothing.** If the ticked name already wraps, or the sentence does not fit on one line, the slip is exactly the plain slip and nothing is said or kept (`secFit()` measures it after each draw and when the phone is turned). The slip never grows, Undo keeps its place and its 44 px, and no line on the page moves. Measured in Barlow on a 390 px phone: one-word sections fit, at Large and Extra large text too; "Fryers are done. Hot larder has 1 left." fits; any sentence with "Salads and dessert" in it does not (it fits a tablet).
- **Not a control.** A tap on the sentence does nothing, so it cannot put the slip and its Undo away by mistake. A tap on the slip's own words, or on the empty room beside the sentence, still does.
- **Where it does not show.** A section of one line, the tick that finishes the whole list on All (the card under the list speaks), the Functions tab, the wall board, the Prep room view, another day, the PIN screen or any other page.
- **Known limits.** A section finished early, then filled again, says nothing the second time that day. If another phone's new line reaches this phone a moment after the server's answer, the words can show for that moment and then go; they are not kept or read out. Whether the words read well with the kitchen's own section names is worth asking three staff.

## The finished words on a wide screen

Built 11 November 2026 (owner: "yea lets go", after the service council; [the wide-screen study](mascot-desktop-options/brief.md): round 1 Build smaller, 5 of 5; round 2, the full-size scene first for four of six and a mascot beside the book last for all six; built as the blend four of six named). The kitchen works on a 21 to 24 inch screen in every section. On a 1920 x 1080 screen he was 64 x 85 px, the section's sentence was 16 px words for 9 seconds, and a screen kept on its section's own tab never showed him on Prep.

- **A finished section, on its own tab.** From 900 px wide, where the tab's page says "Nothing left to do in Sauce.", it says the slip's own sentence, large: "Sauce is done." and under it "Grill has 1 left." He stands under his words as a still, 96 x 128 px under two lines of 40 px words, and never taller than his words: 64 x 85 px under 28 px words, 48 x 64 px under one line of 40 px words, and not there under one line of 28 px words. The tail of the speech shape points down at him and grows with the words (14 px at 40 px words). The page's own "See Done today under Handy." stays under him at the book's size. What is done is in the green that means done; what is left is in the page's ink.
- **The finished list.** From 900 px wide the card says "Every line ticked. 6 of 6." the same way, and only there is he his drawn size, 192 x 256 px, with the one Serve play he had. While its last ticked line still lingers it is the small card; the large words come when the list has emptied, on the draw he speaks and plays on, so their size is chosen once. A line the same person ticked on their other screen lingers here too, and the card draws the page again itself when that linger ends.
- **A state, with the slip's own rules.** One function makes the sentence for the slip and for the page (`secSaid()` in `js/prep.js`), so they cannot differ: today's own list, two lines or more, every one ticked and on the server, nothing waiting to be sent, the names the slip does not say. Where the slip is silent the tab keeps its plain words, so "Sauce is done." stands alone only when the whole list is finished. It is worked out on every draw: another screen's tick changes "1 left", a new line takes it away, ticking it brings it back. It comes with the server's answer (`secDue()`), not at some later draw.
- **Words first.** `finFit()` measures after each draw, and when the window or the signal changes. One size of words for both finished states on a screen: 40 px from 1280 px wide where the room in view above the slip holds them, 28 px otherwise, and the book as it was where neither fits. The room is taken with the window where it rests, so on a window shorter than the book the size does not turn on how far it is scrolled. Then the largest mascot that still fits, then none. The words follow the Text size setting. Long section names wrap inside the page at the screen's size of words; they drop a size only when the wrapped words do not fit. They are the page's own and never wait for his file or his art. While either is on its way his room is kept, so nothing moves when he arrives, and the tail waits for him; when either fails the words stand alone, with no empty room where he would be. When the signal comes back his file gets one new try, since a finished page can stand for hours.
- **A still on a section's tab.** No play there, even for the tick that finishes the whole list: Serve stays with the finished list's card. A play asked for under the finished list never starts or carries on on a tab the cook opens meanwhile: the tab gets a fresh still. Not a control: a tap does nothing.
- **Quiet with no signal.** While the book knows it has no signal the finished words give way to the plain ones and the small card, and come back with the signal. It knows two ways: the browser says so, or the list's own last answer came from memory and not the server (the wifi up and the internet down), which the browser does not see.
- **Never under a row.** While removed lines a chef has asked to see stand on the list page, the tab keeps its plain words and the list its small card; Hide removed brings the large words back.
- **Nothing else changes.** The slip keeps its sentence at every width, so for up to 9 seconds the same words stand on the slip (with Undo) and in the page. Nothing shows beside a list with a line left. No line, tab or button moves. Under 900 px the book is exactly as it was, and nothing of the mascot is fetched on a section's tab there.
- **Known limits.** A screen kept on the All list sees nothing new until the whole list is finished. Once tonight's lines for tomorrow are written and they are the only lines left, no section can be named for them, so the finished words give way to the plain ones (the slip's own rule). With every line of the day's own list ticked, "Sauce is done." stands alone even while a function's platters are open on the Functions tab, as the card says "Every line ticked" of the day's own list. When the same person is signed in on two screens and ticks a section's last line on the other one, the struck line stays on this screen's tab until its next draw (older than this change, in `docs/open-work.md`), so the finished section's words wait for that draw; the finished list's card ends that linger itself. A shared kitchen screen locks 10 minutes after the last touch, so the finished words are seen for at most 10 minutes there. Nothing draws the page at Queensland midnight by itself, so finished words can stand until the next draw, as the finished list's card can. On a 1920 x 1080 screen zoomed to 150% his room on a section's tab is within a few pixels of holding him at 96 x 128 px, so with the kitchen's fonts he is that size or steps down to 64 x 85 px there. How it reads from 1.5 m on a real section screen has not been checked.

## Contract

- **Files.** `js/mascot.js` (lazy: loaded by `mascotSync()` in `js/app.js` on the first named Home view, `LK_MASCOT.api` 1; not in `SHELL` or `js/upkeep.js`). Art in `art/mascot/`: 11 compact sprite sheets (`<action>-sheet-compact.webp`, 4 x 2 frames of 192 x 256 px) and 11 stills (`<action>-still.png`), 3.6 MB together.
- **Not in the saved copy.** Art loads with `?v=1`. `sw.js` keeps no same-origin file with a query string unless it is a lazy `.js` file, so mascot art never collects in the offline copy. With no signal and no art, the mascot is left out and Home is unchanged.
- **Identity.** `LK_PREP.whoId()` gives the staff id while the person's name shows. The record is per staff id, so two people on one device each have their own set; a lock or handover unmounts it.
- **Saved on the phone.** `lk.mascot.<staff id>`: the actions left this round, the last one shown, the kitchen day it was drawn and whether it has played. Never out of date: the day stamp only gates a new draw. If storage is refused, the record lives in the page's memory.
- **The kitchen day** is `LK_TIME.day()` (Queensland), so midnight starts the next action.
- **Used up only when seen.** An action counts as played only when its play reaches the end. A hidden screen, missing art, no room or a redraw part way through leaves it unplayed, so a later Home view the same day plays it.
- **Reads and writes.** None. No listener, no Firestore, no rules.
- **The finished prep list.** `js/prep.js` loads `js/mascot.js?v=2` the first time a finished list shows (`finPaint()`, through `LK_APP.load`), and uses `LK_MASCOT.sprite()`: a still mascot in a `span.mascot-still` that can play one action once. The span is moved into each redraw, so a play is never cut or started again, and it ends by itself when the span leaves the page. `lk.fin.day` keeps the kitchen day he last played on this device: out of date on the next kitchen day. A failed download, or an answer without `sprite()` (the saved shell hands back an older saved copy with no signal, since lazy files share one saved address), leaves the words and waits for the next time the card shows: one try, never a loop, and a new try on each visit to a prep page (`finFresh()`). A second run of the file is a no-op once a copy with `sprite()` is here, so Home's mascot is never orphaned. The finish is read out (`liveSay()`) once the ticked lines have left, whether or not the file loads. The server's answer to a tick changes no line, so the prep listener only patches the page (`patchOrDraw()`): `finDue()` asks for a draw when that answer is the one the card was waiting for. `LK_MASCOT.api` stays 1: the change only adds.
- **The slip's sentence.** No file, no art, no read, no write: `secSay()`, `secWords()`, `secNext()` and `secFit()` in `js/prep.js`, `.p-toast-two` and `.p-toast-say` in `css/app.css`. The page and the sentence share one rule for a line under "Tonight, for tomorrow" (`tonightLine()`). `lk.said.day` keeps the kitchen day and the sections already said on this device (`{ d, s }`): out of date on the next kitchen day, cleared with the other kitchen words at a lock or handover (`privateKey()`, and the tab's own copy with it), and kept in the tab's memory when storage is refused. The Firestore SDK (10.14.1, read at the source) answers a write's promise before its listeners hear, and a listener can keep saying a line is waiting until its stream catches up, so a tick whose own write this phone has seen answered counts as sent (`secAck`, the tab's memory). The words join the slip where it stands, with no redraw of the page; `secWords()` runs inside `draw()` and never throws.
- **The finished words on a wide screen.** No new file, no read, no write: `secSaid()`, `secView()`, `secWaiting()`, `secPage()`, `secDue()` and `finFit()` in `js/prep.js` (`itemsCold` is the list's own word that its last answer came from memory); `.nb-scene`, `.nb-w40`, `.nb-w28`, `.nb-f192`, `.nb-f96`, `.nb-f64`, `.nb-f48`, `.nb-f0`, `.nb-noart` and `.nb-fin-sec` in `css/app.css`, inside a 900 px rule. A finished section's plain sentence carries the finished words (`data-fin-sec`); `finFit()` builds the scene beside it only when the width, the signal, this version's styles and the measured room allow, so an older saved stylesheet or a narrow window shows the plain words. `finPaint()` puts the same still in either scene; the read out and the one play stay with the finished list. Nothing is kept on the device for it.
- **Motion.** One pass, never a loop. A hidden tab or scrolling it out of view ends the play at once on the Welcome pose. Reduced motion never plays automatically and loads no sprite sheet.

## Cost

About 14 KB of script once per device. Per person per day: one still (about 45 KB) and one sprite sheet (250 to 330 KB), from the browser's own cache when already fetched. No Firestore reads. The finished list fetches the Welcome still, and the Serve sheet (292 KB) only on the phone that plays it. From 900 px wide a finished section's tab fetches the same file and still, once per device from the browser's cache; under 900 px it fetches nothing.
