# Queensland menu survey (21 Oct 2026)

This survey read 120 Queensland menus as inspiration for the Kitchen Book's drawing library: 40 quick and casual venues, 40 pubs, hotels and clubs, and 40 restaurants. Each menu was read from the venue's own site, PDF or ordering page on 21 October 2026. The record lists the parts that make up the dishes (meat, seafood, vegetables, sides, sauces, garnish and so on) and how plates are served.

- No prices, costs or supplier details were recorded.
- No venue's dish is copied as a recipe or named in the app. The counts show which parts are common, so the drawings cover them.
- Brand names appear only under "Search words (brands)" at the end. Components carry plain names.
- A component counts once per venue, however many dishes use it. Fruit counts any use, sweet or savoury (pineapple on a burger counts).
- Only what each menu says was recorded. A menu that could not be read in full is marked "thin menu" or "could not read".

How the menus were read: 47 full page, 56 PDF, 6 thin menu, 11 could not read.
